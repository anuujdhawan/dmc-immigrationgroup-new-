## SECTION 5 — Standard Visitor Visa

## SECTION 6 — <a href="http://visit-visa/australia/">Family Visit Visa </a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` If you have close family members living in the UK, you can apply for a family visit visa. This visa allows you to spend time with them during holidays, special events, or just for a short stay. You will need to show proof of your relationship and your travel plans.

## SECTION 7 — <a href="http://visit-visa/australia/">Business Visit Visa </a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` This visa is specifically for those who are planning to visit the UK for work-related reasons. It covers short trips for meetings, attending conferences, or signing business deals. It does not allow you to take a job in the UK. You must show proof of your work purpose and return plans to Abu Dhabi.

## SECTION 8 — <a href="http://visit-visa/australia/">Medical Visit Visa </a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` If you need to visit the UK for private medical treatment or to support someone receiving treatment, you can apply for a medical visit visa. > tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` With a UK visit visa, you can stay in the UK for up to 6 months at a time. This is enough time to explore the country, meet family, attend business meetings, or get medical treatment. It is important to leave the UK before your visa expires.

## SECTION 11 — <span> Visa Validity </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The UK tourist visa from Abu Dhabi is usually valid for 6 months. However, you can also apply for a visa that lasts for 2, 5, or even 10 years. These longer visas allow you to make multiple visits, but you must leave after each visit and cannot stay more than 6 months at a time.

## SECTION 12 — <span> Entry Options </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` It can be a single-entry or multiple-entry visa. A single-entry visa lets you enter the UK once. A multiple-entry visa lets you travel to the United Kingdom several times during the visa’s validity. Most applicants prefer the multiple-entry option for more travel flexibility.

- `<p>` In some rare cases, you can apply to extend your stay up to 6 months. This is usually allowed for medical reasons or if you are a scholar doing academic work. You must apply before your current visa expires and show strong reasons for the extension.
