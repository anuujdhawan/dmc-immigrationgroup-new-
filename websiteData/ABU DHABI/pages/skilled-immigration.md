## SECTION 7 — Skilled Immigration Requirements

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Meeting the legal requirements set by immigration authorities like the IRCC in Canada or the Department of Home Affairs in Australia is very important. Failing to provide the essential documents results in delays or rejection. Our <b>best skilled immigration consultant</b> helps you understand the eligibility rules clearly and supports you in meeting every requirement.
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>A completed and signed Schengen visa application form.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A valid passport issued within the last 10 years, with at least two blank pages.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A detailed travel itinerary that outlines the purpose of your visit, along with proof of your flight reservations and accommodation (such as hotel bookings, rental agreements, or an invitation letter if you're staying with someone).</span>
- `<li>` <span>Two recent passport-sized photos with a neutral expression and a light background.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Proof of financial means, such as bank statements from the last 3-6 months showing enough funds to cover your stay, along with a letter from your employer or proof of income, and possibly a sponsorship letter.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Travel insurance that covers all Schengen countries, with a minimum coverage of €30,000 for medical emergencies and accidents.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A reference letter from your employer or sponsor if you're traveling for business, along with the company’s trade license.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Documents proving your business ownership, like a copy of your trade license.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Evidence of ties to your home country, such as an employment contract or copies of property ownership papers.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A certificate showing you have no criminal record, issued by the UAE police.</span>
