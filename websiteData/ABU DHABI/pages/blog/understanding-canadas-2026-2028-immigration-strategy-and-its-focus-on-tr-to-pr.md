## HERO

- `<p>` Canada has announced its immigration plan for 2026-2028. It sets targets for how many people Canada wants to accept as permanent residents each year and explains. It sets the direction for Canada’s immigration priorities, focusing heavily on helping temporary residents, such as international students, temporary foreign workers, and caregivers, transition to permanent residency, PR.
- `<p>` Canada has long been a favourite destination for people from Abu Dhabi and across the UAE because of its stable economy, quality education, and family-friendly environment. Understanding this new immigration plan can help you identify the right pathways, prepare your documents, and plan your move with confidence.
- `<p>` In this detailed guide, we will discuss what the new plan means, who can benefit, and how you can take advantage of the TR to PR focus.

## SECTION 1 — What Is the 2026–2028 Immigration Levels Plan?

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The 2026–2028 Canadian immigration plan outlines how many new permanent residents it aims to welcome and under which categories. It divides immigration into three main classes. Economic, family, and refugee/humanitarian. The economic class, which includes <a href="https://dm-consultantabudhabi.com/skilled-immigration/">skilled workers</a> and international graduates, remains the largest category. The government clearly states that one of its major goals is to make it easier for people already in Canada on temporary status to obtain PR.

## SECTION 2 — Why Is Canada Focusing on TR to PR?

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Canada is facing an ongoing labour shortage in many sectors, like healthcare, construction, and technology. Temporary residents already working or studying there are perfectly placed to fill these gaps because they’re familiar with Canadian workplaces, culture, and lifestyle. So, instead of bringing in completely new workers from abroad, the government wants to help those already in the system settle permanently. It will reduce the adjustment period and ensure a smoother integration into the economy.

## SECTION 3 — Who Can Benefit Most from This Plan?

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` All Abu Dhabi residents or nationals who have completed their studies at a Canadian institution and gained work experience through a Post-Graduation Work Permit have a clearer path to PR.
- `<p>` Those employed under work permits, particularly in high-demand sectors, can also benefit from fast-track PR options.
- `<p>` Individuals providing essential services, such as healthcare aides, also have access to straightforward routes to permanent status.
- `<p>` The family class will continue to offer opportunities for reunification, allowing spouses and children to join loved ones already settled in Canada.

## SECTION 4 — Main Pathways for TR to PR

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` There are several well-established routes that support the TR to PR transition. Here’s an overview of the key ones.
`<ul>`
- `<li>` Express Entry or Canadian Experience Class
- `<li>` Provincial Nominee Programs or PNPs
- `<li>` Post-Graduation Work Permit, PGWP
- `<li>` Caregiver Programs
- `<li>` Pilot and Regional Programs
`</ul>`
