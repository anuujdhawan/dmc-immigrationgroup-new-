## SECTION 2 — RNIP Canada Consultants

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Initiated by the Canadian government, the Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot (RNIP) is a community-driven program to boost the economy of smaller communities in semi-urban and rural areas. RNIP Canada creates a way to acquire permanent residence for skilled foreigners who want to work and live in one of the eleven participating communities. These communities include Sudbury, North Bay, Timmins, Thunder Bay, Brandon, Sault Ste. Marie, Altona/Rhineland, Claresholm, Moose Jaw, Vernon, and West Kootenay (Trail, Castlegar, Rossland, Nelson).
