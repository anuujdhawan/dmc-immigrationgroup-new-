## SECTION 6 — How to Apply for Poland Work Permit from Kuwait

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The application process for Poland Work Permit starts with your company, which needs to conduct a Labour Market Assessment. The assessment is to prove to the government that the respective job role can be performed only by a skilled foreign professional and cannot be done by a citizen of Poland or another EU-member state.
- `<p>` The employer is responsible for the candidate’s work permit application and will need to include documentation proving that the following conditions are satisfied:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>The conditions of employment are favourable under all applicable employment regulations, including provisions of the Labour Code.</span>
- `<li>` <span>According to the Voivodeship Office, Remuneration is no more than 30% lower than the average monthly wage.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Labour Market Assessment document</span>
- `<li>` <span>The economic activity of the employer</span>
- `<li>` <span>Company deed or trade license</span>
- `<li>` <span>Services contract being provided in Poland</span>
`</ul>`
