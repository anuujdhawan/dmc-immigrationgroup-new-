## SECTION 4 — Switzerland Visit Visa Features

> tag: `<h2>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span><b>Visa Validity</b> The Switzerland visit visa is issued under the Schengen Visa (Type C) category, which allows you to visit Switzerland and other Schengen countries for a limited period. The validity of this visa depends on your travel history and itinerary. Typically, the visa is valid for 90 days within a 180-day period, but in some cases, it may be issued for a shorter duration.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Stay Duration</b> The Switzerland tourist visa duration is maximum 90 days within a 180-day period. The visa does not allow continuous long-term stays, and travelers must ensure that their visit does not exceed the permitted duration. The immigration authorities at the Swiss border or airport may check the remaining days of your allowed stay, so keeping track of your travel days is essential. If you plan multiple short visits, your total stay within 180 days must not exceed the limit.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Entry Options: Single vs. Multiple Entry</b> Switzerland visit visas can be issued as single-entry or multiple-entry visas, depending on your needs and travel plans. A single-entry visa allows you to enter Switzerland once, and after leaving, the visa becomes invalid. On the other hand, a multiple-entry visa enables travelers to enter and exit Switzerland and other Schengen countries multiple times within the visa’s validity period. If you plan to visit multiple Schengen destinations or travel back and forth between Switzerland and Kuwait, a multiple-entry visa is the best option.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Visa Extensions</b> Extending a Switzerland visit visa is generally not allowed unless there are exceptional circumstances, such as medical emergencies or unforeseen events. You must leave Switzerland before your visa expires and apply for a new visa if you wish to visit again. Overstaying beyond the permitted duration can lead to fines, travel bans, or future visa rejections.</span>
