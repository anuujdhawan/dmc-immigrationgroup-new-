## SECTION 1 — Want to know more?

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Fill out this form. We will connect you back soon!
- `<p>` Recently, the Australian department of state announced some changes to the immigration programs. Among the changes were the replacement of subclass 489 with the 491 Skilled Regional Visa.
- `<p>` It took effect from 16th November 2019.
- `<p>` As a result, South Australia has also modified its State Nominated Occupation List and the respective requirements.
- `<p>` Although there were no changes to the occupations with the “Special Conditions Apply.” However, below are the recent adjustments that have been made to State Nomination Occupation for South Australia.
- `<p>` The minimum points required to apply under some occupations/categories have been increased to either 75 or 85.<br>Nevertheless, international students from South Australia, or individuals who have worked in the region for up to 12 months, require 65 points to be eligible.
- `<p>` Most trade occupations still require a minimum of 65 points.
- `<p>` Due to the recent reduction in the total number of slots available for the subclass 190 visa, extra criteria have been added.<br>These include:
- `<p>` An applicant whose occupation falls under the ‘high-point-category’ will now require 95 points to be eligible<br>In the chain migration class, the minimum eligibility score is now 75.
- `<p>` There is a 10% increase in the cost of application for all state nomination programs in South Australia. The new fees will take effect from 4th December, 2019.
- `<p>` There will be no more addition to the list of State Nominated Programs as soon as the system begins to receive applications.<br>Applications have commenced for the subclass 190 and 491 visas, respectively, in South Australia.
