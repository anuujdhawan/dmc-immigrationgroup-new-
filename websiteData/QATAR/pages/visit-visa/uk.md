## SECTION 5 — Standard Visitor Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Standard Visitor Visa is the most common type of <b>UK tourist visa</b>. It is for those travelling for tourism or personal visits. It allows you to explore the UK’s rich culture, visit family or friends, and engage in short-term business activities like attending conferences or meetings.
- `<p>` To get this visa, you must provide proof of financial stability and accommodation arrangements.

## SECTION 6 — Business Visitor Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The business visitor visa is for professionals who are travelling to the UK for work-related purposes. It allows you to attend meetings, negotiate contracts, and participate in trade fairs. However, it is not suitable for long-term employment or business activities.
- `<p>` It requires an invitation letter from a UK-based organization, proof of professional ties in Qatar and sufficient funds.

## SECTION 7 — Family Visitor Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Do you want to visit family members residing in the UK? A family visit visa is ideal for you. To apply for this visa you must provide proof of your relationship with the UK-based family member including the birth certificates, marriage certificates, and other official documents.

## SECTION 8 — Short-Term Study Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` A study visit visa is necessary to attend a short educational course or training program in the UK. You can enrol in language courses, professional certifications, or specialized workshops that lasts for 6 months only.

## SECTION 9 — Private Medical Treatment Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` A medical visa is issued for 6 months or, in some cases for11 months. It allows you to get medical treatments in the UK. However, to apply for this visa you must provide details of the treatment plan, stay duration, and total cost.

## SECTION 10 — Transit Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` A transit visa is required to pass through the UK when travelling to another country. It is applicable for individuals who need to leave the airport or those whose connecting flights require immigration clearance.
