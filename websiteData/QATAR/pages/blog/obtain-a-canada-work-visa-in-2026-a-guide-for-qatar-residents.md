## SECTION 3 — Employer-Specific Work Permit, Closed

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` This is the most common path for expats in Qatar. With an employer-specific work permit, you are allowed to work for one specific employer who has offered you a job. It means you cannot switch to another company without applying for a new permit. To get this, your future boss in Canada usually has to prove that they could not find a local person to do the work. This proof is known as a labour market impact assessment.

## SECTION 4 — Open Work Permit

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` This is often called the Golden Ticket of migration. An open work permit allows you to work for almost any employer in the country. You don’t need a permanent or temporary job offer to apply for this, but not everyone can get it. Usually, it is reserved for people like the common law partner or spouse of a high-skilled worker, or for international students who just finished their degrees. It is a great option because it gives you the freedom to explore the job market.
