- `<p>` Here are the documents required you need to prepare:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <strong>Valid passport</strong>: It must not be old or damaged and have at least two blank pages for the new stamps.
- `<li>` <strong>Valid travel document: </strong>It shows you can travel safely and legally.
- `<li>` <strong>Employment contract:</strong> This is your signed work contract. It shows your exact salary, your job title, and your weekly hours.

## SECTION 12 — Phase 1: In Poland

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The process actually starts far away in Europe. You cannot do anything until your future employer does their part first.
`<ul>`
- `<li>` First, they must pass a strict labour market test and prove to the government that they really require foreign workers to help their business grow.
- `<li>` Next, the Polish employer submits a work permit application to their local office. The local governor looks at the entire file closely. They check whether the company registration is real and active.
- `<li>` Sometimes, a foreign worker performs daily tasks for a current employer who sends them to Poland for a project. If you quit and get a new employer, you need a completely new permit.
- `<li>` The office checks the rules for all foreign workers, including skilled workers and other foreign nationals. When it is finally approved, they send the original paper to you in Qatar.
`</ul>`

## SECTION 13 — Phase 2: In Qatar

> tag: `<h3>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` Now it is your turn to act. After receiving papers, you must book an appointment at the embassy in Doha.
- `<li>` Fill out the form online very carefully because making any error or even a typing mistake can lead to your appointment cancellation.
- `<li>` Prepare all your documents required to enter the territory of the Republic of Poland safely and honestly. Make sure all your copies are clear.
- `<li>` Attend your appointment on time, give your fingerprints, and submit your file. They check whether you are allowed to enter any EU member states without restrictions and verify that you are allowed to visit other member states.
- `<li>` After you arrive and settle in, you can apply for a temporary residence permit. It gives you a plastic local ID card. Eventually, after many years, you can even seek permanent residence.
`</ul>`
