## SECTION 4 — Eligibility Criterions for The Czech Republic Work Permit

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Czech Republic is one of the few European countries that has an open-door policy for skilled migrants. The eligibility criteria to apply for a Czech Republic work permit are based on the applicant’s nationality, profession, and previous experience. The applicant may be eligible if he/she has a valid residence permit in the Czech Republic and his/her profession is listed in the “List of Professions”.
- `<p>` The applicant may also be eligible if he/she has at least two years of professional experience in their field, with at least one year of that experience being gained in their home country. To qualify for this criterion, the applicant must have a diploma or degree from an educational institution recognized by the Ministry of Education.
