> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` You must have a valid national passport. It should be valid for at least 6 months before your planned travel to Canada, Poland, or the Czech Republic.
- `<p>` A valid job offer is necessary from an employer in the country you have applied for
- `<p>` You must meet the minimum requirements for the position you are applying for.

- `<p>` You must have enough financial support to sustain yourself during your stay in Canada.
