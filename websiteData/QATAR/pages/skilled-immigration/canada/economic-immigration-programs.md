## SECTION 6 — Eligibility Criteria for Canadian PR Under Canada Economic Immigration Program

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` An applicant must have continuous 1-year full-time experience in the relevant field of his/her studies.
- `<p>` The professional work experience of an applicant must be categorized in one of the following skill types/levels:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Skill Type 0 (Managerial occupations)</span>
- `<li>` <span>Skill Level A (Professional occupations)</span>
- `<li>` <span>Skill Level B (Technical occupations and Skilled traders)</span>
`</ul>`
- `<p>` Applicant must secure a minimum of 67 points under the skilled worker point grid. This point grid is composed of the following 6 basic factors with a total of 100 points:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Expertise in the English Language</span>
- `<li>` <span>Level of education</span>
- `<li>` <span>Professional work experience</span>
- `<li>` <span>Age</span>
- `<li>` <span>Arranged employment/Job offers</span>
- `<li>` <span>Sponsors in Canada</span>
`</ul>`
- `<p>` Applicant must go through a language test as per the requirements of the official immigration authorities
- `<p>` Prove enough financial funding for stability in the country
- `<p>` A security background clearance certificate
- `<p>` A basic medical examination from authorized and registered doctors from the Canadian embassy.
- `<p>` The applicant must be 18+ to apply for any of the economic immigration programs
