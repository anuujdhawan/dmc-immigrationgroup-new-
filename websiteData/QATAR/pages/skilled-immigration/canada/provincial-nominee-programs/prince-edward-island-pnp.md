## SECTION 4 — Overview of Prince Edward Island Provincial Nomination Program

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The provincial government works with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) to invite new immigrants to work, study and settle in the province through Prince Edward Island Provincial Nomination Program. Currently, the province prioritises entrepreneurs and candidates qualified enough to work in occupations with identified skill shortages in the labour market.
- `<p>` The PNP in the Edward Island was introduced in 2001 to invite more skilled immigrants to bridge the labour market requirements and lead to economic prosperity of the province. It attracts foreigners with the experience, skills, education likely to establish on their own on the Island.
- `<p>` The province has attracted more immigrants since introducing the PNP in 2001 and the Atlantic Immigration Pilot (AIP) in 2017.
- `<p>` The AIP authorises designated employers in Atlantic Canada to hire foreign skilled workers they could not fill locally. The Atlantic provinces in Canada include Newfoundland and Labrador, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, and PEI.

## SECTION 8 — Eligibility Criteria to Apply for Prince Edward PNP Program

> tag: `<h3>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Be at least 21 years of age</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have a valid passport or travel document</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have proof of your identity</span>
- `<li>` <span>Meet the minimum language requirements of Canadian Language Benchmark level 4 for speaking and listening, and level 3 for reading and writing in either English or French</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have a job offer from a Prince Edward Island employer OR be planning to start or invest in a business on Prince Edward Island</span>
- `<li>` <span>Meet the specific eligibility criteria for the category under which you are applying</span>
`</ul>`
- `<p>` In addition to the above general criteria, there are also specific eligibility requirements for each category under Prince Edward PNP. For example, under the Business Impact category, applicants must have a personal net worth of at least $600,000 CAD and experience owning and operating a business.
- `<p>` Meeting the above criteria does not guarantee that you will be nominated by Prince Edward Island. The PEI Office of Immigration will assess all applications on a case-by-case basis to determine if an applicant is eligible for nomination.
