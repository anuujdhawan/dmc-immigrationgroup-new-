## SECTION 8 — Overview of the Alberta Provincial Nominee Program

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Alberta Nomination Program is a program that collaborates between the Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) and the Alberta provincial government to attract skilled workers from overseas to promote economic development. This program, formally known as AINP and now AAIP is a popular way for skilled workers, investors, and entrepreneurs to work, live, and settle in Canada.
- `<p>` It nominates individuals for Canada PR in Alberta based on their skills and qualifications to address labor market shortages or to create new employment opportunities by starting a business in Alberta. After receiving a nomination, applicants can apply for permanent residency status with their legal spouse or common-law partner and dependent children.
