## SECTION 4 — Overview of the BC Provincial Nominee Program

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The BC Provincial Nominee Program is a joint effort between the provincial and federal governments, providing an opportunity for highly skilled workers in occupations that are in demand, according to the 2021 National Occupational Classification. This program is one of the economic immigration pathways to obtaining permanent residency in Canada. The NOC system categorises jobs based on the training, education, experience, and responsibilities (TEER) they require. Qualified workers and entrepreneurs can utilise this program to obtain permanent residency and settle in the province with their families.
- `<p>` The program has two phases as part of the Canada-British Columbia Immigration Agreement. In the first phase, British Columbia authorities select and nominate skilled foreign workers, talented international students, and accomplished entrepreneurs to meet labour market demands, support government priorities, and promote economic development. Once nominated, you and your family can apply to Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) to become Canadian permanent residents. The province can shortlist you from the Express Entry pool, or you can apply separately to the BC PNP portal.

## SECTION 5 — BC PNP Points Calculator

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` For the BC PNP application, it is essential to understand how the Skills Immigration Registration System (SIRS) works. This intake management system allows British Columbia to select candidates for Provincial Nomination based on their scores. To begin, you’ll need to register a profile and provide information about your job offer in BC, work experience, language ability, and education level.
- `<p>` The SIRS operates on an EOI / ITA basis. Registered candidates are assigned a score of 200, and the BC government conducts periodic draws. Candidates who meet the minimum cut-off for their category in that draw are invited to apply for a provincial nomination.
- `<p>` The SIRS is influenced by vital economic factors, including:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>NOC skill level of the job posting in the province: Maximum 60 points</span>
- `<li>` <span>Annual salary offered: Maximum 50 points</span>
- `<li>` <span>Job Location (Regional District of employment): Maximum 10 points</span>
- `<li>` <span>Directly related work experience: 25 points maximum</span>
- `<li>` <span>Level of education: 25 points maximum</span>
- `<li>` <span>English language proficiency: 30 points maximum</span>
`</ul>`
