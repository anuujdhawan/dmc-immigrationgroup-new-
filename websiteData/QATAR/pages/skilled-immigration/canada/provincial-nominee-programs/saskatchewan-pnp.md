## SECTION 4 — Overview of Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` SINP is a program that aims to attract extremely skilled workers, those experienced in in-demand occupations, workers with job offers, and accomplished entrepreneurs who can start and maintain their businesses in the province. The Canadian federal government has recently enhanced SINP nominations by 42%. In the fourth quarter of 2022, international immigration added 9,764 people to Saskatchewan’s population, a 196.9% increase from the same period in 2021.
- `<p>` For 2023, Saskatchewan received the largest allocation in the SINPs’ history – 7,250 nominations on request. It means around 18,000 immigrants to the province this year, including nominees, spouses, and dependents. The province’s notional allocation 2024 is 8,000 nominees and 8,500 in 2025.
- `<p>` \On May 3, 2023, the SINP conducted a new EOI draw and invited 1,032 candidates. On May 18, the province also held its second draw of the month, inviting 260 candidates under its Occupations In-Demand stream and 784 Express Entry candidates. All candidates required an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA), and both categories required a minimum score of 67.
- `<p>` Candidates seeking Canada PR through Saskatchewan PNP nomination must register an EOI through the SINP online portal. The SINP’s online application system, OASIS, administers program applications and issues correspondence.

## SECTION 7 — Experience Stream

> tag: `<h3>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>You must have a genuine work permit and work experience in Saskatchewan for at least 6 months</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must also have a full-time employment offer from a designated Saskatchewan employer in NOC TEER (Training, Education, Experience and Responsibilities) 0, 1 or 2, or 3; or in a registered trade.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Alternatively, you can work in the Hospitality sector on a temporary work permit through an LMIA (Labour Market Impact Assessment)</span>
- `<li>` <span>Or work for an approved trucking firm in Saskatchewan on a temporary work permit granted by IRCC and based on an LMIA report from the Government of Canada.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Recognised diploma or degree from Saskatchewan or Canada</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 8 — Entrepreneur or Farmer Stream

> tag: `<h3>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Possess a minimum of three years of entrepreneurship experience in the last 10 years</span>
- `<li>` <span>A minimum of CAD 500,000 in Net Business and Personal Assets</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must also be able to invest CAD 300,000 or more in a business in Regina and Saskatoon or CAD 200,000 in other Saskatchewan communities</span>
- `<li>` <span>Produce a Business Establishment Plan</span>
- `<li>` <span>Participate actively in managing company operations</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must create at least two job vacancies for Saskatchewan or Citizens of Canada residents</span>
- `<li>` <span>Sign a valid Business Performance Agreement (BPA) with the government of Saskatchewan</span>
- `<li>` <span>A regular post-secondary diploma or degree of at least two years from a recognised learning institution in the International Entrepreneurship stream and a valid Work Permit</span>
`</ul>`
