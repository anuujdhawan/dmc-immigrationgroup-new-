## SECTION 4 — Choose your Ontario Immigration Program Stream

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` When choosing an Ontario Nominee Program stream, it’s important to understand the variation between base streams and enhanced streams. Enhanced streams are directly aligned with the Express Entry system, which the Canadian federal government uses to manage permanent residence applications for Canada’s economic class immigration programs. Candidates who apply through Express Entry receive a point-based Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score.
- `<p>` The higher-scoring candidates are then invited to apply for permanent residence. If a candidate has a nomination from an enhanced provincial program, they receive an additional 600 points. It significantly increases their chances of receiving an Invitation to Apply (ITA) for Canada PR. In comparison, base streams are not aligned with Express Entry, so candidates must apply directly to Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
