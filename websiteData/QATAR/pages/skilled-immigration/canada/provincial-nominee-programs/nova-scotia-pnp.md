## SECTION 5 — Overview of the Nova Scotia Provincial Nominee Program

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Nova Scotia Provincial Nominee Program is created to attract potential immigrants and address labour market shortages in the region. It was launched in 2001 and selects foreign skilled workers and accomplished entrepreneurs who want to settle permanently in the province. To get eligible for the program, applicants must first give an expression of interest (EOI) through the Express Entry system. If successfully nominated, they will receive an additional CRS 600 points towards their overall Express Entry score and higher ranking on the Comprehensive Ranking System to get invitation to apply (ITA) for permanent residence from Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
- `<p>` The Atlantic province offers numerous job opportunities, particularly in healthcare and infrastructure, making it a popular destination for immigrants. If you have the specific skills and are migrating from Qatar, the NSNP can increase your chances of receiving an ITA for permanent residence.

## SECTION 6 — Eligibility Criteria for Nova Scotia Nominee Program

> tag: `<h2>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>A genuine employment offer from a designated employer in the province.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A minimum one year of experience (or continuous experience) or months of experience in some instances in a relevant TEER category of the NOC.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must have a registered profile in the IRCC Express Entry</span>
- `<li>` <span>Pool with a minimum score of 67.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A verified authority must assess your educational credentials.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must have a settlement plan to permanently show your commitment to living in Nova Scotia, Canada.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Sufficient and legal financial resources to support your settlement in the province.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must have language skills of at least Level 4 CLB in the official language (English or French), ideally reaching Level 7 CLB.</span>
- `<li>` <span>If you’re applying as a business person, the eligibility requirements will depend on your level of investment or senior management experience. You’ll also need an eligible business and a successful current business plan that meets Nova Scotia’s minimum requirements.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must have the required skilled work experience.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You’ll need to meet additional experience and program requirements in certain cases.</span>
`</ul>`
