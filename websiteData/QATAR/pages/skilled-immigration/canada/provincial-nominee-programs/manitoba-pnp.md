## SECTION 4 — Applying for the Manitoba Nominee Program

> tag: `<h3>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>The first step is to create a Canada Express Entry profile and fill up all the necessary fields such as age, education, work experience, etc.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Prove your language skills by attempting the IELTS exam and getting at least a 6 out of 10. Attempt taking the French and English assessments; you can earn extra points if you are proficient in multiple languages.</span>
- `<li>` <span>The Canada PR will be issued once the IRCC has verified your profile. Upon that, you will qualify for the Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program to immigrate to Canada.</span>
- `<li>` <span>To have your education evaluated and verified, acquire an Educational Credential Assessment from an IRCC-designated organization.</span>
- `<li>` <span>As soon as your Express Entry profile is created, you are eligible to have your name considered for the Manitoba Nominee Program and receive a nomination from the province by writing an Expression of Interest. You can then proceed with your application for permanent residence in Canada with IRCC once you have received the Nominee Certificate from the province. </span>
`</ul>`
