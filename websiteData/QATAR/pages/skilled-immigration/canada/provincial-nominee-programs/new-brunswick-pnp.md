## SECTION 3 — Documentations Required to Apply for the New Brunswick PNP Program

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` To apply for the New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program, candidates must first submit an expression of interest through the Express Entry system. Those who are selected from the Express Entry pool will be invited to apply for nomination. Once nominated, candidates can then apply for permanent residence through Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
- `<p>` When applying, individuals will need to provide the following documents:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>A valid passport or travel document</span>
- `<li>` <span>Proof of language proficiency in English or French</span>
- `<li>` <span>Educational credential assessment report</span>
- `<li>` <span>Work experience documentation</span>
- `<li>` <span>Police clearance certificate(s)</span>
- `<li>` <span>Medical examination results</span>
- `<li>` <span>Financial documentation</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 5 — Eligibility Criteria for New Brunswick Canada PNP

> tag: `<h2>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Have at least two years of work experience in a skilled occupation</span>
- `<li>` <span>Score at least 67 points on the New Brunswick self-assessment grid</span>
- `<li>` <span>Demonstrate an intermediate level of English or French proficiency</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have proof of sufficient financial resources to settle in New Brunswick</span>
- `<li>` <span>Be between the ages of 21 to 55</span>
`</ul>`
