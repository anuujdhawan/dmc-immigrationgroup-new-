`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>You must have completed a recognized nursing diploma or degree equivalent to Canadian standards. A Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) is often preferred for registered nurse positions.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Must demonstrate proficiency in English or French through standardized tests like IELTS or CELPIP (English) or TEF (French), meeting the required score levels.</span>

- `<li>` <span>Proof of good health through medical exams and a clean criminal record check to demonstrate your suitability for work in the Canadian healthcare system.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Qualify under immigration programs such as the Express Entry system, Provincial Nominee Programs (PNPs), or other pathways specifically designed for healthcare professionals.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Applicants with strong adaptability factors, such as prior experience in multicultural environments or a spouse with language proficiency, may score additional points under Canada’s immigration systems.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Show evidence of funds to support yourself and your family during the initial settlement period. </span>
- `<li>` <span>Complete a medical examination to verify physical fitness for work in Canada.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Provide a clean police clearance certificate to demonstrate good character.</span>
`</ul>`
