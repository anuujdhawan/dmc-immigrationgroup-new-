## SECTION 2 — Overview of Atlantic Immigration Program Canada

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Since it was first implemented in 2017, the Atlantic Immigration Program in Canada has made significant progress, and it has emerged as a potentially beneficial alternative for those who are considering immigrating to Canada. As part of the Atlantic Canada PNP, talented individuals may be eligible for nomination and a streamlined path through the immigration process for different Canadian Atlantic provinces (Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, and New Brunswick).
- `<p>` The Atlantic Immigration Program enables immigrant candidates who have letter of employment, permit referral letter, and endorsement letter to apply for permanent residency in Canada. Because of the tremendous success of the AIP, the program was made into an ongoing one from the beginning of 2022. Since the program’s commencement have seen an increase in their capacity to meet the immigration quotas established by the AIP, and they currently accept around 6,000 newcomers annually.
- `<p>` You can easily apply for one of the Atlantic Provinces via concerned immigration authority if you successfully meet the standard eligibility criteria for candidates,

- `<li>` <span>Nova Scotia</span>

`</ul>`

## SECTION 4 — How to Apply for Atlantic Immigration Program from Qatar?

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Atlantic Immigration Program is a program that is sponsored by employers to hire foreigners for a specific job post. Every primary candidate who wishes to immigrate to Canada under this program is required to have both a written customized settlement plan for themselves and their families as well as a work offer from a selected Canadian employer.
- `<p>` Once the candidate has shown interest in the position and accepted it, the employer will engage the qualified candidate in touch with a designated settlement service provider organization for the purpose of conducting a needs assessment and developing a settlement plan. The applicant will also get help for their family through their employer so that after they arrive in Canada, they can realize the objectives of their settlement plan.
