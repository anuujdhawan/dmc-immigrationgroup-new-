## SECTION 2 — Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot Program Overview

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Canada northern and rural immigration program has been established by the Canadian government to attract highly skilled foreign workers to remote provinces in the country.
- `<p>` Community involvement is essential to the success of the Canada rural Immigration program. The objective of RNIP Canada program is to broaden the advantages of economic immigration to areas of a more manageable number by establishing a pathway to permanent residency for qualified foreign employees who desire to work and reside in one of the participating communities.
- `<p>` The RNIP program is intended for foreigners who have obtained a referral from a participating community, received an offer of employment within the same community’s limits, meet basic standards regarding education, have professional work experience, expert Language skills, proof of settlement fund, and to reside in the community that preferred them along with all the community requirements.
