## SECTION 3 — Features of Morden Canada Immigration

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` MCDII is a strategic initiative Canada PR pathway with the official support of Morden city. The small city offers new residents good education facilities, economy, and community life.
- `<p>` The <strong>Morden Canada Immigration</strong> program doesn’t require a job offer or sponsorship from family or friends in Canada. The candidate should have a registered profile in the Canada Express Entry Pool, except for a few unique occupations, to be accepted for MCDII.
- `<p>` This program helps to fill gaps in Morden’s labour market. Applicants who do not directly qualify for MPNP can apply through MCDII to fetch a support letter from the province. Applicants with a relevant work experience in the most-demand occupations in Morden get preference.
- `<p>` The most demanding occupations under this program are manufacturing, carpenters, welding, industrial sewing machine operators, and childcare and daycare workers.

## SECTION 4 — Overview of Canada MDCII Program

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Through participation in the MCDII program, prospective immigrants to Canada have a better chance of being granted permanent residency in this province. The candidates are not expected to have a friend or relative living in Morden City at the time of application. All that is required of them is a legitimate registration in the Express Entry system. Additionally, their NOC needs to be included on the highly demanded NOC list of Morden. MCDII is a support program for those who are thinking about applying for permanent residency in Canada. Applicants who are interested in applying but do not meet the requirements to do so on their own can get a support letter from MCDII. The only candidates whose applications will be considered by MCDII are those who have a valid profile in the Express Entry program.

## SECTION 5 — Eligibility Criteria to Apply for the MCDII Program

> tag: `<h3>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span><b>Age:</b> The applicant applying for MCDII must be between the age of 21 & 45 to qualify for the application.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>No Relatives:</b> Unlike other immigration programs, it is not necessary for the applicant to have family or friends residing in other provinces within the country.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Education:</b> The applicant must fulfill the minimum educational qualification as demanded by the specific occupation.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Language Expertise:</b> Since English language proficiency is a must for most immigration programs, it is also necessary for the applicants to clear the IELTS exam in order to qualify for the MCDII application.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Minimum Annual Pay:</b> The applicant should acquire a job offer that pays a minimum of CAD 45,000 annually.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Financial Proofs:</b> The applicant must be able to show proof of funds when applying for the MCDII program.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Professional Work Experience:</b> The applicant should have at least have 2 years of professional work experience in the respective field of studies and the applied positions.</span>
`</ul>`
