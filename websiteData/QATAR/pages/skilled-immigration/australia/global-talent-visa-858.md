> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Global Talent Visa Australia is a special visa program that provides an opportunity to pathway for a permanent residency visa in Australia for skilled individuals with relevant skills, qualifications, and experience. This type of visa is specifically designed for the economic development of the different sectors in smart cities of Australia. The Global Talent Visa Australia offers an opportunity for skilled migrants to apply for Permanent Residence in the country, allowing to work and live there. This visa was introduced by the Australian government to attract the brightest talents from around the Globe to fill skill shortages in certain industries & target sectors in order to strengthen the Australian economy and provide economic benefit to the country.
- `<p>` The Talent Visa Australia is a points-based visa system, where applicants are assessed based on their qualifications, work experience, age, language proficiency, and other factors. The total score that an applicant achieves determines if they will be eligible for this visa. To be successfully eligible for the Global Talent Visa Australia, applicants must have an “approved sponsor” to prove that they are highly skilled in their chosen field of expertise and have the potential to make a significant contribution to Australian society and stronger economy.

This visa also provides holders with the option of applying for Australian citizenship after four years, as well as access to a wide range of government benefits.
