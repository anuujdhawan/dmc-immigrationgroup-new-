## SECTION 2 — Overview of Skilled Regional Provisional Visa Australia (Subclass 491)

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The Skilled Regional Provisional visa (subclass 491) is a points-tested skilled work visa for regional Australia. It permits you to professionally work and reside in designated regional areas of Australia for up to 5 years. After living in a regional area for 3 years, you may be eligible to apply for permanent residence. You must be nominated by an Australian Government agency or state, or sponsored by a family member eligible to apply for this visa. If you are not nominated or sponsored, you may still be eligible to apply for this visa under the SkillSelect criteria if you can meet all other eligibility criteria. The Skilled Regional Provisional Visa is a points-based visa for skilled workers.

## SECTION 6 — How to Apply for Skilled Work Regional Provisional Visa Australia Stream (Subclass 491)

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The first step in applying for this visa is to lodge a subclass 491 visa application form with the department.
- `<p>` Once your application has been lodged, you will be given a case number and an application number. These numbers will be used to track your application through the system. You will also be given an information sheet that will provide further details on the application process.
- `<p>` The next step is to provide evidence of your identity, skills, English language proficiency, and other relevant documentation as required by the department. You will need to provide original documents or officially certified copies of original documents. The department will return all original documents to you once they have been verified.
- `<p>` You may also need to provide police certificates and/or penal clearances as part of the application process. You should obtain these before lodging your application. Police certificates are required for all applicants aged 16 years or over. Penal clearances may also be required in some cases. Please refer to the department’s website for more information on police certificates and penal clearances.
- `<p>` Once you have provided all the required documentation, your application will be assessed by a departmental officer. The officer will consider all the evidence you have provided and decide on your application.
- `<p>` If your application is successful, you will be granted a subclass 491 visa which will enable you to professionally work and live in a specific regional area of Australia for four years. Qualified applicants can apply for permanent residence after they have lived in the regional area for at least three years.
