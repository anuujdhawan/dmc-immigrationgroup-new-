## SECTION 2 — Overview of Partner Dependent Visa Australia

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The Spouse Dependent Visa 820 is a visa for the dependent spouse of an Australian permanent resident or citizen. The holder of this visa is able to live and work in Australia indefinitely. To be eligible for this visa, the applicant must be married to an Australian permanent resident or citizen. The relationship must be sincere and ongoing, and they should reside together. The applicant must also come across health and character conditions.
- `<p>` There is no minimum period of time that the couple must have been together before applying for this visa. However, evidence of the relationship will need to be provided, such as joint bank accounts, shared accommodation, and birth and death certificates if applicable. The Spouse Dependent Visa 820 is a temporary visa that is valid for two years. After two years, the holder can apply for a permanent residence visa if they remain in a genuine and continuing relationship with their partner.
