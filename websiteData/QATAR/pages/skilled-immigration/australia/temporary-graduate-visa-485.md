## SECTION 2 — Overview of Graduate Visa Australia 485

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` If you have completed an Australian tertiary qualification and skills assessment requirements, you may be eligible for the temporary graduate visa 485. This allows you to stay and work in Australia after your studies are finished. The Graduate Work and the Post-Study Work are two different streams available for temporary graduate visa 485.
- `<p>` The subclass 485 visa is a part of the Australia temporary graduate program. The purpose of this student visa program is to give international students opportunities to gain skilled work experience in Australia so they can contribute to the economy under the graduate skilled visa. If everything goes well, those same students may be able to pursue permanent residency through the points-tested skilled migration program. There are several requirements that must be met in order to apply for the subclass 485 visa, including but not limited to having a valid passport, being 18 years of age or older, overseas student health cover, having completed an Australian Qualifications Framework qualification, and skills assessment via skills assessment authorities. Other general requirements include evidence of English competency and meeting health insurance standards. It is also necessary to provide intent documentation proving your plans to live in Australia temporarily while participating in skilled work.
