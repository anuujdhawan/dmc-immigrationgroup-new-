## SECTION 7 — How to Apply for Skilled Independent Visa 189?

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` To apply for an Australia subclass 189 skilled independent visa, you must submit an expression of interest (EOI) through Australia’s Skillselect. It is a must to provide some basic information about your age, English language skills, work experience, and educational qualifications. Furthermore, you might need to provide evidence that you have met the requirements for your chosen occupation.
- `<p>` If you are invited to apply for the Subclass 189 Skilled Independent Visa, you will need to submit a complete visa application, including all required supporting documents. You can expect to receive a decision on your visa application within four to six months.
