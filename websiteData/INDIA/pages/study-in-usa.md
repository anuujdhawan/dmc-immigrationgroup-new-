## SECTION 6 — <span> Passport </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Provide a valid passport or travel document that remains active for at least six months beyond your intended stay in the United States. It must be in good condition and have a blank page for stamping.

## SECTION 7 — <span> Form I-20 </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Submit the original, signed Form I-20 if applying for an F-1 or M-1 visa. This document is issued by your chosen school.

## SECTION 8 — <span> DS-2019 Form </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you are applying for a J-1 visa, you need this form. It serves as proof that you are officially accepted into a certified program.

## SECTION 9 — <span> Visa Application Form </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Complete and print the DS-160 online application form. Make sure you read the instructions carefully before filling out the form to reduce the risk of errors.

## SECTION 10 — <span> Fee Payment Receipts </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` You have to show proof of your SEVIS fee and payment. It registers your information in the Student and Exchange Visitor Information System, which tracks your visa and study status while in the United States. You must also pay the application fee and attach receipts to your file. The overall student visa USA price varies depending on the type and duration of the program you are enrolled in.

## SECTION 11 — <span> Photograph </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Provide one recent color photo that meets the specifications listed on the Department of State website. The background should be white, and it must be taken within the last six months.

## SECTION 12 — <span> Interview Appointment Letter </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` You need to carry the interview confirmation page that highlights the date and time of your visa interview. It verifies that your appointment has been successfully scheduled with the embassy or consulate.

## SECTION 13 — <span> Financial Documentation </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` You need to provide recent bank statements and other relevant papers to prove your ability to pay tuition fees and living costs. You can also provide sponsor letters, proof of scholarships, or education loan approvals.

## SECTION 14 — <span> Academic Certificates </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Bring all relevant transcripts, diplomas, and certificates from previously attended institutions. It helps reflect your academic readiness and your commitment to continuing studies.

## SECTION 15 — <span> Standardized Test Reports </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Submit the official score reports of any tests required by your school, such as IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, GMAT, or SAT.

## SECTION 16 — <span> Proof of Ties to Home Country </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Provide evidence that shows your intention to return to India after completing your studies. Property documents, family certificates, or future employment offers can serve as valid proof.

## SECTION 17 — <span> Travel Itinerary </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Prepare and attach a copy of your travel plan. It must outline your expected date of arrival and the address where you plan to stay in the United States.

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If your spouse or children are applying with you, you should include their passports, relationship proof, and separate application forms. Each dependent must also pay the required fees and attend their interview.
