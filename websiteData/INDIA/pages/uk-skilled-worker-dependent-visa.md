## SECTION 2 — What is a UK Skilled Worker Visa for Dependents?

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` This program is designed for individuals whose partners are working in the UK. It helps you join them and live together while they build their career. It means skilled worker visa UK holders can sponsor their dependent family members, such as a spouse, civil partner, or dependent children, to move to the country legally. This opportunity helps families stay united while enjoying the lifestyle and benefits the UK offers.
- `<p>` As a dependent, your stay and rights in the UK are directly linked to your partner’s skilled worker sponsorship and immigration status. Once approved, you can live, study, and even work in most roles during your stay. It’s a great way to settle with your partner and explore future opportunities together. You cannot apply under this category if your partner holds a temporary work permit, such as a seasonal worker visa.
