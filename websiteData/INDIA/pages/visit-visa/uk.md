## SECTION 13 — UK Visit Visa Options

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The UK government offers different types of visit visas depending on the purpose of travel. ## SECTION 14 — Standard Visitor Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Standard visitor visa is the most common visa type for Indian travellers visiting the UK for leisure, tourism, or short-term business purposes. It allows you to stay in the UK for up to 6 months and can be used for visiting friends and family, attending events, or even short educational courses. The <b>UK tourist visa</b> does not permit long-term stays, work, or access to public funds.

## SECTION 15 — Family Visit Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you have close relatives residing in the UK, you will need a Family Visit Visa to visit them. This visa is a subcategory of the Standard Visitor Visa but is specifically for those travelling to see family members. However, you must prove your relationship with the host in the UK and show that they have enough financial means to support their stay.

## SECTION 16 — Business Visit Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` For Indian professionals travelling to the UK for business meetings, conferences, training sessions, or investment opportunities, the business visit visa is the ideal choice. This visa allows short-term business activities but does not permit employment or long-term business operations.

## SECTION 17 — Medical Treatment Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you need to travel to the UK for private medical treatment, you can apply for a Medical Treatment Visa, which falls under the Standard Visitor Visa category. This visa is granted to individuals who require specialized treatment at a UK hospital or medical institution. You must provide medical reports, proof of treatment arrangements, and financial evidence to support your stay.

## SECTION 18 — Short-Term Study Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Indian students planning to take a short course, training program, or research study in the UK can apply for a Short-Term Study Visa. This visa is valid for courses lasting up to 6 months or 11 months for English language courses and does not allow work or long-term academic enrollment.

## SECTION 19 — Transit Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` For Indian travellers passing through the UK en route to another country, a Transit Visa may be required. This visa is necessary if you need to change airports or have a layover in the UK exceeding 24 hours.

## SECTION 21 — Stay Duration

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` The UK tourist visa from India typically allows a stay of up to 6 months per visit, depending on the purpose of travel. For certain visa types, such as the short-term study visa for English language courses, travellers may be granted a stay of up to 11 months. It is essential to adhere to the permitted stay duration to avoid any future visa complications.

## SECTION 22 — Visa Validity

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` While most standard visitor visas are valid for 6 months, the UK also offers long-term visit visas with validity periods of 2, 5, or 10 years. However, even with these long-term visas, each visit must not exceed 6 months at a time.

## SECTION 23 — Entry Options

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` The UK visa can be issued as either a single-entry or multiple-entry visa. A single-entry visa allows you to enter the UK once, while a multiple-entry visa lets you visit the UK multiple times within the visa’s validity period. If you are a frequent traveller, applying for a multiple-entry visa can make your trips more convenient.

## SECTION 24 — Visa Extension

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` In general, the UK visit visa cannot be extended beyond 6 months, except in special cases such as medical treatment visas or research studies. If you require a longer stay, you must apply for an appropriate visa extension before your current visa expires.
