## SECTION 13 — Visitor Visa or Subclass 600 – Tourist Stream

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Tourist Stream of an Australian visit visa from India is designed for individuals who want to visit Australia for tourism purposes, sightseeing, or meeting friends and family. This visa allows you to stay for up to three, six, or twelve months, depending on the conditions of approval. So, if you’re planning a holiday, attending events, or visiting loved ones, this type of visa is an ideal option for you.

## SECTION 14 — Business Visa (Subclass 600 – Business Stream)

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you’re traveling to Australia for business meetings, conferences, or professional engagements, the Australia Business visa Stream(Subclass 600) is the right option. This visa allows business professionals to attend official meetings, explore business opportunities, or participate in trade events, but it does not permit working in Australia.

## SECTION 15 — Sponsored Family Stream

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you are an Indian citizen who has family members or relatives residing in Australia, you can apply for the Sponsored Family Stream under the visitor visa. This visa requires an Australian citizen or permanent resident to sponsor the applicant, providing financial support and assurance of their return.

## SECTION 16 — Medical Treatment Visa (Subclass 602)

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` For Indian travelers seeking medical treatment or consultations in Australia, the Medical Treatment Visa (Subclass 602) is the right choice. This visa is granted to individuals needing medical care, organ donation procedures, or support for a patient traveling to Australia for treatment.

## SECTION 17 — Transit Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you’re an Indian traveler transiting through Australia on your way to another destination, you may need a Transit Visa (Subclass 771). This type of Australia visit visa for Indian residents is specifically designed for short stopovers, allowing you to pass through Australia for up to 72 hours without requiring a full visit visa.
