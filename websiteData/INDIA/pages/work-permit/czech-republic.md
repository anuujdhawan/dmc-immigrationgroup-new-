> tag: `<h3>`

The eligibility criteria to apply for a Czech Republic work permit are based on the applicant’s nationality, profession, and previous experience. The applicant may be eligible if he/she has a valid residence permit in the Czech Republic and his/her profession is listed in the “List of Professions”.
