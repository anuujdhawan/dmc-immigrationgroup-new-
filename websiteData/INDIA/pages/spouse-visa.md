## SECTION 4 — Who is Eligible for a Canada Spouse Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Not everyone is eligible to apply for a dependant visa Canada. The Canadian immigration authorities have strict rules to make sure every relationship is legally recognized and genuine. Both the sponsor and the sponsored person must meet eligibility requirements to qualify for this program.

## SECTION 5 — Eligibility Criteria for the Sponsor

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you are planning to bring your partner to Canada, you must meet the following criteria:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>You must be a person holding citizenship Canada, a permanent resident living in the country, or a person registered under the Canadian Indian Act.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must be currently residing in Canada or show valid proof that you will return once your partner arrives.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You cannot sponsor anyone if you are a temporary resident or do not hold a valid immigration status.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Your relationship must be legally recognized. You have to prove it through official documents such as a marriage certificate, joint financial accounts, or shared rental agreements.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must have written proof that your relationship is genuine, especially if your marriage or partnership took place in a non-visa-exempt country.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must meet the minimum income requirement set by the government to prove that you can financially support your partner without relying on social assistance.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must not have any serious physical or mental condition that prevents you from fulfilling your responsibilities.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must not be in prison, under a removal order, or declared bankrupt at the time of application.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must not have an unpaid immigration loan or any other government debt that affects your eligibility.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must not have sponsored a previous spouse or partner in the last 3 years.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must not have been sponsored by someone in the last 5 years.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must be ready to sign an undertaking agreement, confirming that you will support your partner for at least three years after they arrive.</span>
- `<li>` <span>If you are living in Quebec, you must meet additional regional conditions and sign a province-specific undertaking form.</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 19 — Check Eligibility

> tag: `<h3>`

## SECTION 20 — Collect Documents

> tag: `<h3>`

## SECTION 22 — Fill Out the Forms

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Download and complete all required forms from the official IRCC website. Make sure the information in your forms matches your supporting documents. Any mismatch can lead to clarification requests from the visa officer.

## SECTION 23 — Pay the Fees

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` You will have to pay the sponsorship fee, processing fee, and permanent residence fee before submitting your file. You must keep the receipts safe, as they serve as proof of payment.

## SECTION 24 — Submit the File

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` After filling out all the forms and paying the fees, it’s time to submit documents and the application. Incomplete files or missing documents may result in delays or rejection.

## SECTION 25 — Provide Biometrics

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Once your application is received, IRCC will send a biometric instruction letter. Visit the nearest Canada Visa Application Centre (VAC) in India to complete biometrics. You may also need to undergo a medical exam by a panel-approved doctor.

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Your application will be reviewed to verify your relationship and check the sponsor’s eligibility. If your application is approved, you will receive a passport request. Submit your passport to the nearest visa office or VAC for stamping.

> tag: `<h4>`
