## SECTION 4 — Who is Eligible for a Partner Resident Visa NZ?

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` You can apply for a partner of resident visa for NZ only when you meet certain eligibility requirements set by Immigration New Zealand. Here’s everything you need to qualify for under this program.
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>You must be in a genuine and stable relationship with your partner. Your relationship can be a marriage, civil union, or de facto partnership.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You and your partner must have shared a home for at least 12 months before applying. You will need to show proof, such as joint bank accounts, shared bills, same address for mail, joint ownership of property, or rental agreements, to confirm your relationship is real and ongoing.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must be at least 18 years old to apply. If you are 16 or 17, you will need written consent from your parents or legal guardians.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must meet all health and character requirements, which include providing medical and police certificates to show you are in good health and have no criminal history.</span>
