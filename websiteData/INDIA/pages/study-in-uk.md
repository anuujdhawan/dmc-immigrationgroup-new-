## SECTION 4 — Who Can Apply for UK Student Visa?

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` You need more than just the urge to study abroad to qualify for this visa. You need to meet all the criteria set by the UK Home Office, such as:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>You must be at least 16 years old at the time of your application. If you are under 18, you will need parental consent from both parents or legal guardians. It confirms your permission to study and live independently in the UK.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You need an unconditional offer from a licensed education provider. It means the institution where you plan to study must hold an official sponsorship license from the Home Office, proving it is authorised to teach international students.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Your course must be valid under the immigration rules. It should be a full-time program at RQF level 3 or above. In some cases, it can be a pre-sessional English language course or a postgraduate program. Part-time study is allowed only in limited situations.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must receive a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from your college or university. It includes your personal and course details, and your visa application must be submitted within six months after receiving it.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must have enough money in your bank account to pay your first year’s tuition and cover your living expenses. The required funds must be available for at least 28 consecutive days before applying. The amount needed depends on the city you will study and stay in.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must show your ability to speak, read, write, and understand English at the level needed for your course, such as CEFR Level B2 for degree-level study or B1 for lower-level courses.</span>
- `<li>` <span>If you are already studying in the UK and applying for a new visa, your next course must show academic progression. It generally needs to be at a higher level than the previous qualification unless an exemption applies.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must be a genuine student with clear academic goals. You may be asked to attend an interview to prove your study intentions and show that you plan to leave the UK once your visa expires.</span>
- `<li>` <span>If you are from a country where tuberculosis testing is mandatory, you must hold a valid TB test certificate issued by an approved medical centre.</span>
- `<li>` <span>If you are applying for certain scientific or engineering programs at the postgraduate level, you need an Academic Technology Approval Scheme certificate before applying.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must confirm that you will not depend on public funds or government benefits during your studies in the UK. </span>
`</ul>`
