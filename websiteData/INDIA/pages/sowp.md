## SECTION 2 — What is SOWP in Canada?

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The SOWP full form is spousal open work permit. It allows the spouse or partner of a person holding a valid work permit, a valid temporary resident visa, or permanent residency in Canada to work legally during their stay. It offers a great opportunity for families to stay together while building a stable future abroad.
- `<p>` If your partner is a temporary foreign worker or international student living in Canada, you may also qualify under this program. It helps qualifying foreign nationals maintain emotional and financial stability, as they can live with their loved ones.
- `<p>` Whether you’re married or in a common-law relationship, the SOWP makes it easier to enter Canada and live together under a temporary visa. This program is designed to support couples who want to balance career growth and family life.

## SECTION 4 — Who Can Apply for the Canada Spousal Open Work Permit from India?

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Not everyone can qualify for a visa under this program. Your eligibility requirements for the Spousal Open Work Permit depend on your spouse’s status in Canada and the type of authorization they currently hold. In general, you must meet the following eligibility criteria:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Your partner or principal applicant must hold a valid permit and be employed in a high-skilled occupation under TEER 0 or 1 categories.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You can also qualify if you are the spouse of a worker in selected TEER 2 or 3 jobs, such as healthcare, construction, and natural resources. If your partner is in TEER 4 or 5 roles, you cannot apply for SOWP.</span>
- `<li>` <span>The work authorization of your spouse must remain valid for at least 16 months from the date you apply.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Your partner must be currently employed in Canada or have a confirmed job offer.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must show a genuine relationship with your partner to prove your application is based on real family ties.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Certain undergraduate study program fields, like nursing, education, and science, may also qualify if they meet IRCC requirements.</span>
- `<li>` <span>If your partner is a study permit holder, you can apply only if they are a full-time student at a Canadian-designated learning institute. However, it applies mainly to those applicants whose partner is studying for a master's or doctoral degree. You can also qualify if they are enrolled in long-term professional programs such as medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, veterinary medicine, law, and engineering.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You can qualify if your partner holds a post-graduation work permit and works in an eligible TEER category occupation.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Both you and your spouse must live together in Canada throughout the application process.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You can also apply if your spouse is part of the Atlantic Immigration Program, working in essential or skilled occupations.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Your partner maintains legal status in Canada, or has applied to extend or restore it before expiry.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Applicants under any category must meet Canada’s general admissibility rules, including good health and no criminal record.</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 31 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">Is SOWP the same as a bridging open work permit?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` No, they are different. The SOWP is specifically designed for spouses or common-law partners of foreign workers or students. Meanwhile, a Bridging Open Work Permit (BOWP) is for individuals who have already applied for permanent residence and are waiting for a decision.

## SECTION 32 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">How long can I stay in Canada on a SOWP?</a>
