## SECTION 4 — Skill Assessment

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Before you can even talk to the government, you must prove you can do the job. You need to get a skill assessment from the right authority in Australia. They will look at your degrees and your past work to ensure you meet Australian standards.

## SECTION 5 — Max Points Strategy

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Do not lodge your interest the moment you have 65 points. Instead, work on a strategy to get more. Can you retake your English test? Can you wait a few months for another year of experience? Getting higher points makes your Australia work visa application stronger.

## SECTION 6 — EOI Submission

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Once you have your assessment and English results, you go to the Home Affairs website and submit an Expression of Interest. This is not the visa; it is just you telling Australia that you want to apply. There are no visa fees at this stage.

## SECTION 7 — Invitation Rounds

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The government holds rounds where they pick the best candidates. Once you are invited, you become one of the skilled workers nominated for a permanent role. Make sure to submit your complete application within 60 days.

## SECTION 8 — Visa Lodgment

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` This is the final step. You will fill out the visa application form, pay the Australia work visa fees, and upload all the required documents. They may also ask you to provide your identity documents, a valid passport, and police clearance certificates. Go through health examinations and medical examination tests to prove you are fit. The government will also ask for health assessments for any family members joining you.
- `<p>` During this time, you will receive a transaction reference number to track your case. The processing time can vary, but usually, it takes several months. You will need to show bank statements and other required documents to prove your claims. Unlike a temporary visa, allowing a stay of up to four years or a holiday visa, once you get this visa grant, you are a permanent resident. You won’t need to obtain sponsorship from a sponsoring employer because you already have the right to work in Australia for any company you choose.
