## SECTION 4 — Malaysia Work Visa Options

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The Malaysia Immigration Department issues different types of work visas for Indian citizens based on the nature of employment, duration of stay, and your professional background. Our team of expert visa consultants helps you identify the most suitable work visa category. - `<p>` A <b>Malaysia work visa for Indians</b> is mainly categorized into 5 groups.

## SECTION 5 — Employment Pass

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` It is the most common type of work visa issued to skilled professionals and individuals who have a job letter to work as a manager, technician, or professional in Malaysian companies. To apply for this job, you must earn RM 3,000-5000 per month.
- `<p>` This visa is usually issued for one to five years and can be renewed. It also allows you to bring your spouse and children under the dependent pass.

## SECTION 6 — Temporary Employment Pass

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` As indicated by the name, this visa is for short-term work opportunities in Malaysia. It is issued for middle-level workers and can be further divided intothree categories:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span><b>Category 1</b>: It is for general workers who wants to work in manufacturing, construction, or plantation sector. </span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Category 2</b>: Domestic helpers who are hired to work in Malaysian households need to secure this visa to live and work in Malaysia.</span>
- `<li>` <span><b>Category 3</b>: It is specifically for expatriates who are hired on a temporary contract for semi-skilled roles.</span>
`</ul>`
- `<p>` Each category has its own salary requirements and duration limits. It is usually valid for two years only and cannot be converted into a long-term pass.

## SECTION 7 — Professional Visit Ppas

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` This pass is for Indian professionals who are travelling to Malaysia for short-term assignments, training, or consultancy work. It is usually valid for up to 12 months and does not allow you to work as a full-time employee in the country. This pass is considered ideal for engineers, consultants, trainers, or technical staff visiting on behalf of their employer in India.

## SECTION 8 — Digital Nomad Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Also known as the DE Rantau Nomad Pass, this visa is introduced for remote workers and freelancers. Indian IT professionals, content creators, and digital marketers can apply for this visa to earn a stable income and work with clients outside Malaysia. It is valid for 12 months and can be renewed.

## SECTION 9 — PLS@Xpats or Social Visit Pass

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` This type of work visa is suitable for individuals who wish to stay in Malaysia for a longer period without permanent employment. It is issued to foreign professionals, retirees, or dependents of Employment Pass holders. Though itnot allow full-time work, you can used itfor business-related visits, attending conferences, or exploring future employment options.
