## SECTION 3 — Overview of Morden Community Driven Immigration Initiative

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` MCDII is a strategic initiative between the Canadian city of Morden, Manitoba, and the federal government which acts as a pathway for permanent residence in Canada. This program helps to bridge labour gaps in the local market and add to its economy.
- `<p>` The<strong> Community Driven Immigration Initiative </strong>doesn’t require an employment offer or sponsorship from relatives in Canada. The candidate should have a Canada Express Entry profile, except for a few unique occupations, to be accepted for <strong>MCDII Canada</strong>.
- `<p>` These targeted occupations are:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Welders</span>
- `<li>` <span>Carpenters</span>
- `<li>` <span>Cooks</span>
- `<li>` <span>Plumbers/Pipefitters</span>
`</ul>`
- `<p>` Manufacturing, childcare and daycare workers, and industrial sewing machine operators are also in-demand occupations in Morden.
- `<p>` Candidates who are not directly eligible for MPNP can apply indirectly through MCDII to receive a support letter from the Manitoba province for PR application. Applicants having relevant work experience in one of the most in-demand jobs in Morden will get preference<strong>.</strong>

## SECTION 6 — Minimum Requirements for MCDII Canada from India

> tag: `<h3>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Applicant’s age should be between 21 and 45 years</span>
- `<li>` <span>Should have no other connection to other parts of Canada through friends, relatives, or previous education or employment</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have completed a post-secondary school education or training program of minimum qualifications with a one-year duration</span>
- `<li>` <span>Given General IELTS test with a minimum score of five in each band</span>
- `<li>` <span>Show strong commitment and ability to establish themselves and settle in Morden economically</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have settlement funds as required by MPNP</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have two years relevant work experience in welding, recent full-time work experience in carpentry, or possess experience or previous experience as a cook, plumber, or pipe fitter.</span>
- `<li>` <span>For other occupations, the candidate has additional requirements:</span>
- `<li>` <span>Should have a federal express entry profile</span>
- `<li>` <span>Require a CLB language score of 7</span>
- `<li>` <span>Post-secondary level education that is supported with an Educational Credential Assessment.</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 8 — Application Process for Canada PR through MCDII

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Check your eligibility for Modern’s community-driven immigration initiative.
- `<p>` Create your express entry profile and share it in the Canada Express Entry pool.
- `<p>` Apply through MCDII with a Federal Express Entry profile number and submit all the required documents to get a support letter.
- `<p>` If your profile gets shortlisted, you will receive an interview letter for a detailed exploratory visit to Morden. After the exploring visit, an MPNP officer will interview you, and you can get an invitation to apply.
- `<p>` With the MCDII support letter, you can apply for MPNP. If successful, Manitoba will give you a Nomination Letter to apply for permanent residence in Canada.
- `<p>` Apply for PR and wait for Canada Express Entry Canada PNP Draws and IRCC final decision.
