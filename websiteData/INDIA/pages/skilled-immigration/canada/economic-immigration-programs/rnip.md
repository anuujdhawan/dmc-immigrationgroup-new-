## SECTION 3 — Overview of Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot Program

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` <strong>RNIP Canada</strong> is an economic immigration pilot program that acts as an easy pathway for permanent residence in Canada for skilled foreign workers with the required abilities, educational qualifications, and work experience to boost the economy in rural Canada and create employment opportunities.
- `<p>` It is a community-driven program to improve the economy of smaller communities in rural and semi-urban areas by creating a permanent residency path for skilled overseas workers who want to work and live in one of the participating communities. This initiative is based on the community recommendation to encourage the migration of skilled workers by adding points in the main express entry pool.
- `<p>` The RNIP is designed for foreign workers who intend to live in the specific community for a more extended period and, if approved, are granted the eligibility to apply for permanent residency and an LMIA-exempt Canadian work permit.

## SECTION 4 — Provinces that Participate in RNIP Program Canada

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Launched in 2020 when the Covid-19 pandemic was about to hit the world, the<strong> RINP program for Canada</strong> welcomed 390 immigrants in 2021. It tripled in 2022, allowing 1,360 newcomers to settle in eleven eligible communities.
- `<p>` Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) extended the program until February 2024. IRCC uses the 2021 version of the National Occupational Classification (NOC) for this program. The RNIP is modeled on the popular Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP). The pilot program aimed at fulfilling community requirements may become permanent, like AIP, and is currently active in five provinces in Canada.

## SECTION 5 — Key Features of RNIP Program Canada

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The five-year Canadian Federal Immigration implemented in 2020 helps smaller communities with labour gaps due to skill shortages and aging or low population density to invite and retain new skilled immigrants.
- `<p>` The selection of communities for the<strong> Canada Rural Immigration Program </strong>is based on the following community criteria:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>The population should be at most 50,000, and the location should be at least 75km from the Metropolitan city.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Population to be at most 200,000 and remote location from major cities.</span>
`</ul>`
