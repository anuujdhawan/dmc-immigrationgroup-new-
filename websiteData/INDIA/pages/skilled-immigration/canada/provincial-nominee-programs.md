## SECTION 12 — Overview of Provincial Nomination Program Canada

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The provincial governments operate the program in coordination with the Canadian Federal government. You can apply for the Provincial Nominee Program through the PNP Canada Express Entry or Non-Express Entry process. The province will shortlist a successful PNP applicant from the Express Entry pool or respond to an Expression of Interest to nominate for Canada permanent residency application with the Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). It is the most straightforward pathway for Canadian immigration from India.
- `<p>` This program is for overseas workers who have the required skills, educational qualifications, and relevant work experience to add to the economic growth of a specific province or territory. The applicants should show commitment to living in that particular province and want to become permanent residents of Canada.
- `<p>` Recent IRCC data shows that <a href="https://www.cicnews.com/2023/04/ircc-over-half-of-express-entry-invitations-went-to-pnp-candidates-in-2022-0434170.html"><span>19,160 invitations to apply</span></a> went to candidates in the PNP streams in 2022. The total number of ITAs granted was 37,315, meaning 51% of ITAs in 2022 went to PNP candidates.

## SECTION 15 — Eligibility Criteria for PNP Program Canada from India

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The eligibility requirements vary from province to province according to immigration programs and eligibility criteria set by a specific province in various streams. For example, age and annual earnings requirements for British Columbia PNP vary from that of Ontario or Saskatchewan PNP programs. The general criteria for qualifications are:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Required skills, permanent full-time valid job offer, and in-demand occupations</span>
- `<li>` <span>Canadian work experience or relevant international experience</span>
- `<li>` <span>Required educational level in Canada or foreign educational credentials satisfying Canadian education standards</span>
- `<li>` <span>Proof of language proficiency, such as a language test result card meeting the Canadian language benchmark.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Meet minimum scores or cut-off scores on the comprehensive ranking system of the Express Entry process</span>
- `<li>` <span>The Principal applicant must have the ability to add to the economy of a specific province or territory</span>
- `<li>` <span>Commitment to living permanently in the Canadian province.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Sufficient settlement funds or proof of financial support for a dependent child</span>
- `<li>` <span>No severe health problems or criminal record</span>
`</ul>`
- `<p>` Please note that the Federal skilled trades program management authorities also ask for other supporting documents, like a Provincial nomination certificate, and a job seeker validation code, before making the final decision.

## SECTION 18 — Express Entry process

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Canada Express Entry PNP has two ways to apply:
- `<p>` Contact your chosen province or territory to apply for a nomination certificate under an Express Entry stream.
- `<p>` The provincial government will check profile details and put you among eligible candidates, and if you emerge as a strong candidate, select you among a pool of candidates.
- `<p>` If nominated, create or update an Express Entry profile to show you have been nominated.

## SECTION 19 — OR

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Create an Express Entry profile and choose the provinces and territories you are interested in for immigration.
- `<p>` If a province or territory in Canada sends a “notification of interest” to your account, contact them directly and apply to their Express Entry stream.
- `<p>` If nominated, the province or territory will send a nomination certificate to your account, which you can accept electronically.
- `<p>` In both cases, an applicant must
- `<p>` Satisfy the eligibility requirements of the province or territory and
- `<p>` Share your Express Entry profile to show that you meet the minimum criteria, including your eligibility for one of the immigration programs.
- `<p>` If you are invited to apply (ITA), you submit an electronic resident application to the IRCC website, formerly known as Canadian Citizenship and Immigration (CIC). Select the mode of application for submitting the application form and pay the application fees. Successful applicants can turn temporary status into permanent resident status through Provincial Nomination and become eligible to become Canadian citizens.
