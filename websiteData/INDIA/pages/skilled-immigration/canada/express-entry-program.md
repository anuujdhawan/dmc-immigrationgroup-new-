## SECTION 3 — Overview of the Canada Express Entry

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Canada is regarded as a top destination for immigrants worldwide, and more than one lakh Indians have migrated to the North American country every year in recent times. The country offers high quality of life, security, safety, numerous job opportunities, quality education, and advanced healthcare for permanent residents. <strong>Canada immigration express entry</strong><br>is the central system through which Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) processes immigration applications.
- `<p>` The system was started in 2015 by the Canadian government to fast-track permanent residence applications for economic immigrants.
- `<p>` Candidates make an expression of interest (EOI) – a tool used to manage selection under particular immigration programs. It can select migration candidates for multiple programs, at various skill levels, for a stay in Canada. If the candidates are eligible for one of the economic immigration programs, they enter the Express Entry pool. The federal and provincial governments and designated employers in Canada can pick candidates from this pool who get an Invitation To Apply (ITA) for Canada immigration under one of the programs.

## SECTION 5 — Express Entry Canada Points Calculator

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` It applies a points-based system, CRS, to rank candidates’ profiles. Indian citizens with high CRS cut-off score are more likely to get an Invitation to Apply. Receiving an ITA means they can now apply for permanent residence. After receiving an application, an IRCC officer evaluates it and decides if the candidate’s application is approved.
- `<p>` Foreign professionals must score at least 67 points out of 100 points. CRS cut-off score is used to rank candidates based on the below factors:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>English language proficiency: 28</span>
- `<li>` <span>Educational Credential Assessment: 25</span>

- `<li>` <span>Job offers: 10</span>
- `<li>` <span>Sponsorship in Canada (Relatives): 10</span>

`</ul>`
