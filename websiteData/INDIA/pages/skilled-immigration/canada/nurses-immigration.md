## SECTION 15 — Eligibility Criteria for Indian Nurses

> tag: `<h2>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>A recognized nursing degree or diploma from an accredited institution in India.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A valid Registered Nurse (RN) or Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) license from your home country.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Pass the National Nursing Assessment Service (NNAS) evaluation to verify your credentials in Canada.</span>

- `<li>` <span>One to two years of nursing experience in a hospital or clinical setting.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A valid work visa or permanent residency status can be obtained through Express Entry or Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP).</span>
- `<li>` <span>Complete a bridging program if your qualifications do not fully meet Canadian standards.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Pass a medical examination and background check as part of the immigration process.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Must obtain the minimum points as per the latest CRS score criteria.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A valid job offer letter stating that you are invited by a registered employer in Canada.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Your job must be on the registered occupations list.</span>
`</ul>`
