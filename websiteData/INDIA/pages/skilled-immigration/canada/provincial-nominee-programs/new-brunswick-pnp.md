## SECTION 3 — Overview of Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot Program

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program provides a safe pathway for immigrants to secure permanent residence in Canada. Located in the Atlantic region of Canada, New Brunswick has a large area but a small population, which provides numerous job opportunities for immigrants and a low cost of living.
- `<p>` The economic immigration program is designed for immigrants with the required skills, qualifications, and work experience to positively impact the province of New Brunswick. The immigration program is run in partnership with the Canadian Federal Government, which allows the Government of New Brunswick to nominate candidates for permanent residence in Canada who have the potential to create a brand image for themselves in the province.
- `<p>` Candidates must meet the province’s requirements to qualify for the New Brunswick PNP Program, which include having a valid job offer from an NB employer, a firm intention of living and working in Brunswick permanently, and a willingness to contribute to the province’s economic growth.

## SECTION 12 — Application Process for New Brunswick PNP from India

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` First, submit an EOI that shows you meet the eligibility criteria for a particular stream. The government of New Brunswick will review all EOIs and select candidates based on the province’s economic needs.
- `<p>` If your EOI gets selected, you can move on to the next stage, which involves submitting an online application for NBPNP nomination. Your application will be evaluated, and if successful, you’ll receive a nomination certificate.
- `<p>` Finally, attach this certificate and apply to the Government of Canada for a PR visa via IRCC. Your application will be processed through the electronic management system, and the final decision on issuing PR lies with IRCC.
