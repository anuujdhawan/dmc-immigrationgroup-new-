## SECTION 3 — Overview of the Alberta Provincial Nominee Program

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Alberta has merged among top destinations for immigrants through Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP), earlier known as Alberta Immigrant Nominee Program (AINP). <strong>AINP Alberta</strong> operates through a partnership between Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) and the Alberta provincial government to attract overseas skilled workers for economic development.
- `<p>` This Canada’s popular immigrant recruitment program opens the gateway for overseas skilled workers, investors, and entrepreneurs to work, live, and settle in Canada. It is one of the economic immigration programs that nominates people for Canada PR in Alberta based on their skills and related qualifications to fill shortages in the labour market or create employment opportunities by starting a business in Alberta. After receiving the nomination, you can apply to the federal government for permanent residence status with a legal spouse or common-law partner and dependent children.

- `<li>` <span>Post-secondary education in Canada</span>
