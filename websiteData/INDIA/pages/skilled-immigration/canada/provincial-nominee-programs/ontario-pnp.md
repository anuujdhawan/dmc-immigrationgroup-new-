## SECTION 2 — Key Features of Ontario PNP Programs

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The Ontario Immigration Nominee Program or Ontario Provincial Nominee Program is devised to attract foreign nationals who wish to live, work, and settle in the Canadian province. Although Ontario is the most developed province, it’s still facing shortages of skilled professionals. The Ontario Provincial Nominee program plays an important role in helping the province to meet the demand for skilled tradesmen. The main feature of the Ontario PNP Program is – the candidate cannot be able to apply for the PNP program, until and unless they have a full-time job offer from an Ontarian employer or they need to obtain their doctorate from the Ontario university.
