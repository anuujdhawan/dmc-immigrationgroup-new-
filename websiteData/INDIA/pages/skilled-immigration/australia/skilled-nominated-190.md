## SECTION 3 — Overview of Australia Skilled Nominated Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Skilled Nominated Visa 190 is a point-based immigration program where the applicant needs to score at least 65 points to proceed with the nomination. Further, your profession must be listed in the Combined Skills Occupations List (STSOL – MLTSSL). Australian state or territorial government agency nominates you, which adds to your points and helps you to qualify for a skilled nominated visa under the Australian Department of Home Affairs.
- `<p>` Subclass 190 Skilled Nominated Visa allows people to live and work in Australia and boost the country’s economy. This skilled immigration program, like Subclass 189 Skilled Independent Visa, is based on points, expression of interest, nomination, and invitation to apply, which will get successful approval on sponsorships by employers in Australia.

## SECTION 4 — Key Features of Subclass 190 Skilled Nominated Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` 190 Skilled nominated visa is for highly skilled overseas workers whose fields of expertise are mentioned on the Skilled Occupations List (SOL) in Australia. The applicant must finish the skills assessment test from an authorised authority to get nominated by a state or territorial government agency in Australia. It helps candidates secure more points after EOI.
- `<p>` You can stay permanently in a specific region in Australia and relocate to a different area if needed. The visa comes with a travel component like other Australian skilled visas that expires 5 years after the visa issue date. If you are outside after five years of visa duration, you will need a Resident Return visa to re-enter Australia as a permanent resident.
- `<p>` Families, especially life partner, can be included in this visa application. This permanent visa costs AUD 4,240 for the primary applicant. There is an extra charge for every eligible member of your family who applies for the visa with you. The processing time can range from 3 months to 3 years as the Australian Department of Home Affairs assesses applications based on individual circumstances.
