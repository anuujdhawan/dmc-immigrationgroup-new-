## SECTION 3 — Overview of Temporary Partner Visa Australia

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The family stream predominantly comprises partner visas, allowing professionals in Australia to stay with families and provide them with pathways to citizenship. To be eligible for this temporary spouse visa, the applicant must be either an Australian Citizen, a permanent residence holder, or a New Zealand Citizen. The applicant must have a genuine relationship with a spouse or de facto partner.
- `<p>` To apply for a spouse dependent visa, the sponsor must provide a written commitment to support family members financially during their stay in Australia. It includes – travel expenses, accommodation, and other living expenses. You can apply for separate subclasses of partner visas for temporary stay and then convert it to permanent visa after meeting the eligibility requirements.
