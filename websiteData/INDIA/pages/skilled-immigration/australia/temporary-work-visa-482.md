## SECTION 3 — Overview of Temporary Skill Shortage Visa

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Australia’s skilled migration program aims to attract highly skilled workers from around the world to meet skills shortages in the Australian economy. The program focuses on occupations that are in high demand and where there is a shortfall of suitably qualified and experienced Australians. The subclass 482 visa is for skilled workers who want to come to Australia to work in their nominated occupation for an approved business. This visa involves a two-stage process, firstly, nomination by an approved business and secondly, application by the skilled worker.
- `<p>` The temporary work visa 482 visa has three streams. The short-term stream is for international talent or highly specialized temporary workers doing short term highly specialized work; the Medium-term stream is for international talent or highly skilled workers doing medium-term work; and the Labor Agreement stream is for workers in occupations that have been agreed by the Australian Government as being critical to an industry or sector.
- `<p>` Applicants for the subclass 482 visa must be nominated by an official business. The business must demonstrate that they are unable to source suitably qualified and experienced Australians to fill the position. To nominate a position, businesses must first lodge a nomination application with the Department of Home Affairs. If this is approved, they will then be able to sponsor skilled workers for a subclass 482 visa.
