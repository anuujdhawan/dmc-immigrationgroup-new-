## SECTION 3 — Overview of Australia Temporary Graduate Visa 485

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` This visa is for international students who have recently graduated from an Australian Institution or have required skills and qualifications that are relevant to Skilled Occupation List (SOL) Australia. This Australia Visa acts as a permanent visa pathway for international graduates under skilled work visas. This visa lets you live and work in Australia for 18 months to 4 years by availing of <strong>Aus Graduate Visa</strong>.
- `<p>` International students can now hope to stay longer in Australia after the Australian Department of Home Affairs (DHA) announced plans to increase the duration of stay for some Temporary Graduate visas (subclass 485) from July 1, 2023. With this extensive list initiative, DHA wants to address the problem of skills shortages in key industries and rebuild the sector of international education. All doctored graduates, and certain international graduates with a Bachelor, Honours or Master’s degree will be eligible for Temporary Graduate Visa Extension with a further validity for two years if their qualification is listed on the Eligible Qualifications List of the Australian government’s department of education.
- `<p>` The applicants submitted between July 1, 2022, to June 30, 2023 no longer need to nominate an occupation from the SOL and get skills assessment under different streams of Temporary Graduate visas.

## SECTION 6 — Eligibility Criteria for Australia Temporary Graduate Visa

> tag: `<h2>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Age below 50 years</span>
- `<li>` <span>Present inside Australia on a valid visa</span>
- `<li>` <span>Must have held an eligible student visa in previous six months</span>
- `<li>` <span>Recent qualification in a Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Students (CRICOS) registered course.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Adequate health insurance to cover the costs of you and your family members. You can have private health insurance and your health insurance provider can either be in Australia or in your home country.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Nominate the visa stream and meet additional requirements. You can’t change the stream after you apply</span>
- `<li>` <span>Attach specific proof with the application</span>
