## SECTION 2 — Overview of Australia Skilled Independent Visa 189

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` <strong>Subclass 189</strong> is a permanent resident visa program that permits globally skilled professionals to work, study and stay in Australia permanently. The applicant’s professional occupation should be listed on the Priority Migration Skilled Occupation List (PMSOL) for this substantive visa. It is a points-based visa program that requires a candidate to successfully perform a skill test and meet the eligibility score to be invited to apply by the government/state territory.
- `<p>` This skilled migration Australian visa lets invited workers live and work permanently anywhere in Australia. Indian residents with exceptional skills can apply through the points tested stream. New Zealand citizens and eligible Hong Kong or British National (Overseas) passport holders can apply for a separate subclass of visa. Skilled Independent Visa helps to fill shortages in the Australian labour market.

## SECTION 3 — Features of Australia Visa Subclass 189

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Australia has a strong economy, multicultural society, and vibrant culture to emerge as a popular immigration destination for highly skilled workers. These immigrants want to upscale their careers in a professional and competitive environment, create more opportunities, and earn higher salaries. In return, Australia also benefited from these highly talented, skilled professionals by contributing to the growing economy, filling labour gaps, and creating more employment opportunities.
- `<p>` This visa allows invited professional workers with the required skills to live and work permanently anywhere in Australia. To qualify, a candidate must be under 45 years of age. The applicant doesn’t require a sponsor or nominator for <strong>Visa subclass 189</strong> and must be under 45 to get an Invitation to Apply (ITA). You can be inside Australia or outside when making the application.
