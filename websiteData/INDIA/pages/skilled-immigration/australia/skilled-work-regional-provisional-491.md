## SECTION 2 — Overview of Skilled Work Regional 149 Visa

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` 149 visa is for skilled individuals nominated by a state or territorial government to work and live in designated regional Australia. You can also get sponsorship from an eligible family member living in a designated area. Most locations outside Australia outside significant cities (Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane), like Perth and Gold Coast, are designated regional areas for migration.
- `<p>` The applicant must be nominated to apply by a state or territorial government agency or sponsored by a close relative who is an Australian citizen, resident, or eligible New Zealand citizen. It is a points-tested system from the relevant field in the skilled occupation list. The candidate gets invited to apply after a suitable skills assessment for the occupation.

## SECTION 3 — Key Features of Australia Visa Subclass 491

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Australia is a popular option among immigrants, offering all the required facilities. To welcome more immigrants, the Australian Department of Home Affairs invites skilled professionals through the best-skilled immigration programs that help them to live, study, and work in Australia along with their families.
- `<p>` <strong>Visa Subclass 491 </strong> is a state or territory-nominated provisional visa for skilled immigrants which authorises holders and their families to work and live in a particular Australian region for up to five years. You can become eligible for Australia PR after three years of stay through this visa type. It is a temporary visa like a Partner visa (subclass 820), which can act as a bridging visa for the permanent visa.
- `<p>` Nomination by a state or territory helps a candidate earn more points to qualify for this skilled visa monitored by the Department of Home Affairs points test. It is also called <strong>family sponsored visa 491</strong>, as your relatives can sponsor you to come and settle in a specific region in Australia. The relative must be an eligible citizen or resident of Australia. The applicant must get an invitation to apply (ITA) after giving an Expression of Interest (EOI) in the listed skillset, depending on the specific region. This visa can act as the pathway to apply for permanent residency visas in the form of Skilled Regional Subclass 191. You must withdraw your nomination to be granted a family sponsored visa.

- `<p>` The regional government authorities can nominate you based on your occupation, and you will get an ITA.
- `<p>` After getting the Australian state nomination, you can boost your score and submit your immigration application to the DHA with the necessary documents.
- `<p>` Once you receive the ITA, you need to submit your application for the visa within 60 days by clicking on the link.
