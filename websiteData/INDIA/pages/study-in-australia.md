## SECTION 5 — Important Entry Tests Required to Study in Australia

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Before applying to study in Australia, you need to pass certain tests that prove your language skills and maintain satisfactory course progress. It helps Australian universities to make sure that you can understand lectures, complete assignments, and communicate effectively in English. - `<p>` Here are the main tests you may need to take.

## SECTION 6 — <span> IELTS </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The International English Language Testing System, or IELTS, is one of the most common tests accepted by Australian universities. It checks your listening, reading, writing, and speaking skills. Most universities require an overall band score between 6.0 and 7.5, depending on your chosen one course. Preparing for IELTS helps you improve your English for both academic and everyday communication.

## SECTION 7 — <span> TOEFL </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The Test of English as a Foreign Language, TOEFL, is another widely accepted option. It measures your ability to use and understand English at the university level. You can take it online, and it’s accepted by most Australian institutions. The score requirements are similar to IELTS, usually between 80 and 100 out of 120.

## SECTION 8 — <span> PTE Academic </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Pearson Test of English, PTE, Academic is also accepted for studying in Australia. It’s completely computer-based and gives quick results. Many students prefer PTE because it’s convenient and accurately measures language skills. Universities usually require a score between 50 and 65, depending on the program.

## SECTION 9 — <span> GMAT and GRE </span>

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you’re planning to study business or postgraduate programs, you may need to take the GMAT or GRE. These tests evaluate your analytical, quantitative, and verbal reasoning skills. Not all universities require them, but good scores can strengthen your application and help you stand out.
