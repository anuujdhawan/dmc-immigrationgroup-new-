## SECTION 13 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">What is the main difference between Residency by Investment and Citizenship by Investment Programs?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` Residency by Investment and Citizenship by Investment help secure legal status in a foreign country. However, there are some main differences:
- `<p>` Passport: Temporary or permanent residents cannot apply for a passport in the host country. One can apply for a passport after acquiring citizenship.
- `<p>` Minimum Stay Requirement: There is a minimum period (days) one needs to be in the country to maintain the residence status. However, there are no residency requirements for citizens.<br>Duration: Citizenship is a permanent status, given for life. Future generations can inherit it as well. Residency, on the other hand, is temporary. Residency by Investment usually grants temporary residence permits.
- `<p>` Legal Status: Residency allows individuals to enjoy healthcare, education, and quality living. Citizenship is the highest level of legal status in a country. Citizenship by Investments allows successful applicants to get all citizen rights and vote in elections too. Residents, on the other hand, do not have the right to vote.
