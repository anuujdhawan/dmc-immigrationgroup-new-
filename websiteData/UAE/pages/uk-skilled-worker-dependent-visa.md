## SECTION 4 — Eligibility Requirements for a UK Skilled Worker Dependent Visa from Dubai, UAE

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Before applying for a dependent visa, you must meet eligibility criteria set by the UK government. It ensures that only genuine dependents of skilled workers can join them in the UK. You need to meet the following requirements to become eligible for this program.
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>You must prove that you share a genuine and subsisting relationship with the main visa holder. </span>
- `<li>` <span>You and your partner must be married, in a civil partnership, or an unmarried couple who have lived together for at least two years. The relationship must be real and ongoing at the time of the application.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must show that you can be financially supported during your stay in the UK without relying on public funds. The main visa holder’s employer can certify this on the Certificate of Sponsorship. </span>
- `<li>` <span>If you are not sponsored, you must have personal savings to meet the minimum financial requirement for a partner, first child, and each additional child.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Your partner, who is the main applicant must already hold, or be applying for, a valid skilled worker visa. The dependent visa will be linked to their status and will remain valid for the same duration. </span>
- `<li>` <span>You must show the sponsorship reference number proof from your partner’s Certificate of Sponsorship.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must intend to live together with them in the UK. A dependent partner or child can stay away from studies, but their main home should still be with the family.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must follow all current immigration rules and policies.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Dependent children are eligible to apply if they are under 18 years old and not married or in a partnership. In some cases, a dependant child over 18 may also qualify if they were previously granted permission as a dependent and still live with their parents.</span>
`</ul>`
