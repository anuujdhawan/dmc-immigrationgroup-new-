## SECTION 8 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">Who can you call skilled workers?</a>

## SECTION 9 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">Who qualifies for Australian Skilled Migration?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` The General Skilled Australian Migration program is for people not sponsored by an employer (who have a job offer from an Australian company). Applicant must be above 18 and below 45 years old, with good English language ability, qualifications equivalent to Australian standards, and recent relevant work experience in skilled profession.

## SECTION 10 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">Can you explain what migration skill assessment means in Australia?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` Migration Skills Assessment is a process that assesses the skills of candidates who are applying for visas under skilled immigration with the Australian Department of Home Affairs. The assessment determines whether or not the candidate possesses the required expertise and skills for their nominated occupation listed on the Skilled Occupations List (SOL) in a specific region or area of Australia.

## SECTION 11 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">What is Federal Skilled Worker Program in Canada?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` The Canadian government uses the NOC (National Occupational Classification) to describe different occupations. This list provides valuable information, including job descriptions and education requirements for various Canadian jobs. The NOC is also helpful for employers who need to create job postings and for government agencies to identify skills shortages in the Canadian job market. The list is updated every 5 years to ensure accuracy.
