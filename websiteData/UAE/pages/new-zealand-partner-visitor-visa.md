> tag: `<h2>`

- `<li>` <span>You must be in a genuine and stable relationship with your partner. INZ will expect evidence that you are living together and share a long-term commitment similar to a marriage or civil union.</span>
- `<li>` <span>You must provide detailed relationship evidence, such as joint tenancy agreements, social media posts, joint bank accounts, or letters from friends and family confirming your relationship.</span>
- `<li>` <span>If you are legally married, you must submit a valid marriage certificate as part of your supporting documents. De facto partners have to provide proof of shared responsibilities.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Your partner must be eligible to sponsor your visa. It means they must either be a New Zealand citizen, resident, or hold a valid work or student visa that allows them to support a partner.</span>

- `<li>` <span>You must have enough financial resources to support yourself during your stay in New Zealand, or your supporting partner must agree to cover your living costs.</span>
