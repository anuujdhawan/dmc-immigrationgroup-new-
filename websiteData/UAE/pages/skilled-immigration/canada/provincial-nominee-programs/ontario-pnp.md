## SECTION 9 — How to Apply for Ontario PNP Programs?

> tag: `<h3>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Check your eligibility to apply for an Ontario PNP program. If eligible, send an Expression of Interest (EOI) to the Ontario Province.</span>
- `<li>` <span>The province will assess your profile, and if it meets all the requirements, you will receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA) via email.</span>
- `<li>` <span>After receiving the ITA, you must submit the online nomination application within 90 days. You need to attend an in-person interview to sign the performance agreement. And then, you will receive a Letter of Confirmation from the province.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Contact the IRCC with this letter to get a temporary work permit. Then, you can apply for a provincial nomination.</span>
- `<li>` <span>After being nominated by the province, you can apply for a Canada Permanent Residence within six months.</span>
`</ul>`
