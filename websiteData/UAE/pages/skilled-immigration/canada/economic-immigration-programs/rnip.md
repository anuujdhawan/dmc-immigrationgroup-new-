## SECTION 4 — Key Features of RNIP Program Canada

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` The RNIP program for Canada launched in 2020, welcomed 390 immigrants in 2021. In 2022, the program tripled, allowing 1,360 foreigners to settle in 11 eligible communities. This program Canadian Federal Immigration program initiative is for five years and assists smaller communities with gaps in the labour market due to shortages in skill, aging, or low density of population to attract and retain fresh skilled immigrants. The selection of communities for the Canada Rural Immigration Program is based on specific community criteria:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>It has a population of no more than 50,000 and is located at least 75km from the metropolitan city.</span>
- `<li>` <span>The population should be at most 200,000 and located remotely from significant cities.</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 5 — Eligibility Criteria for Rural Immigration Canada Programs

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Eligibility criteria for this Rural Immigration Canada programs depend on the Training, Education, Experience, and Responsibilities (TEER) required for a particular occupation listed in the NOC 2021. To be eligible, you must meet the following requirements:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Have a secondary school diploma in Canada, post-secondary certificate, or university degree</span>
- `<li>` <span>Be at least 18 years old</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have a valid employment offer that matches the community’s needs</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have relevant work experience in a qualifying occupation</span>
- `<li>` <span>Be proficient in the language used in the community</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have sufficient settlement funds</span>
- `<li>` <span>Have a firm intention to live in the community for a long time and contribute significantly</span>
- `<li>` <span>Meet all community-specific requirements</span>
`</ul>`
- `<p>` If you meet these requirements, you may be eligible to apply for Rural Immigration Canada.
