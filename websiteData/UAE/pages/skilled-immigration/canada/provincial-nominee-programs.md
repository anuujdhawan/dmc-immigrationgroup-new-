**Q** `<h4>` — What is provincial nominee program?

**A** `<p>` — The Provincial Nominee Programs in Canada allow skilled professionals to be selected by specific regions to settle there. These regions nominate individuals who can contribute to their economic development and fill gaps in the labour market for consideration in Canada’s Permanent Residency applications.

**Q** `<h4>` — Is Canada inviting aspirants in 2022 under PNP?

**A** `<p>` — Yes, on May 10, 2023, 589 invitations to apply for a Canada PR were issued under the PNP programs with CRS cut off 691. The previous PNP draws in 2023 were held on March 1, 2023 with CRS cut off 748, and February 15, 2023 with 699 ITAs issued and CRS cut off 791. To get a Canada PR from Dubai, get help from DM Immigration Consultants in Dubai.

**Q** `<h4>` — Does PNP Nomination guarantee PR?

**A** `<p>` — No. PNP nomination doesn’t guarantee PR. A nomination certificate through a PNP program gives qualified candidates a boost to their PR application and increases chances to rank higher with additional CRS points (600 maximum). It acts as a pathway for Canada PR. You must still apply to IRCC and receive permanent resident status to live in Canada permanently. IRCC must be convinced that an applicant with a nomination certificate from a province meets health, financial, and security requirements.

**Q** `<h4>` — What is the difference between PNP programs and Canada Express Entry?

**A** `<p>` — Canada Express Entry is the central process to manage Canada PR applications electronically. PNP programs are run by specific provinces in association with the central government, which form the most accessible pathways to seek Canada PR. Express Entry fast-tracks PR applications within 6 months but follows a complex process with a requirement of high CRS score to rank higher. Base PNP streams in Canada focus on the province’s skills and relevant experience in highly demand occupations.
