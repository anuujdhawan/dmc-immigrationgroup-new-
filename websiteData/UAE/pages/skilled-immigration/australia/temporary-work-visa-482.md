**Q** `<h4>` — What is a Subclass 482 visa?

**A** `<p>` — The Subclass 482 visa, also known as the Temporary Skill Shortage Visa, enables employers to sponsor international workers for up to 4 years. It enables skilled workers from outside Australia to work in occupations that are currently experiencing skill shortages in Australia. This visa can be either short-term or medium-term.
