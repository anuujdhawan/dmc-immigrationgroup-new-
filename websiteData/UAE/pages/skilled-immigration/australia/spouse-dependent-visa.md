**Q** `<h4>` — Who is Eligible for an Australian Dependent 820 Visa?

**A** `<p>` — The 820 Dependent Visa was created to help immigrants with parents and grandparents who are over 65 years of age. This visa is valid for five years and will allow recipients to stay in Australia indefinitely, as long as they can show that they can support themselves financially. Additionally, there is no set limit on the quota of these visas, and applicants must be permanent residents of Australia or have close ties to the country to be eligible.

**Q** `<h4>` — How I can get a Permanent Partner Visa?

**A** `<p>` — The Spouse Visa 820 permits the de facto partner or spouse of an Australian Citizenship holder or PR holder, or an eligible New Zealand Citizen to live in Australia. And this is the first step toward a Permanent Spouse Visa (Subclass 801)

**Q** `<h4>` — Can you work legally in Australia under an 820 Spouse Visa?

**A** `<p>` — The Subclass 820 visa is granted for the spouse or de facto partner of an Australian Citizen/PR Holder/New Zealand Citizen. This visa allows them to live, ad work in Australia for a period of 5 years.
