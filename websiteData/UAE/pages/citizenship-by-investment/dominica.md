- `<p>` The country mainly relies on tourism, agriculture, and forestry. Dominica is one of the leading producers of bananas and other fruits globally. Dominica is a politically stable country and has a democratic government. Dominica is a member of the Caribbean community, Organization of Eastern Caribbean States, and the United Nations. It is part of the British Commonwealth.
