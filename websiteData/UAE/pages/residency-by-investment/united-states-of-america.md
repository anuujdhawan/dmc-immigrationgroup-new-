## SECTION 4 — Eligibility Criteria for US EB 5 Visa

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Here are the <strong>US EB 5 visa</strong> requirements:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>The investor must be 21 years old.</span>
- `<li>` <span>The investor must prove that he has collected the investment funds via legal sources and has the documents.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Investment of $1,000,000 into a non-targeted employment area project or invest $800,000 into a targeted employment project with a high employment rate or in a rural area.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Create 10 full-time long-term jobs for qualified workers in the USA.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Successful applicants get a Green Card to live in the USA with family.</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 6 — Selecting an EB-5 Project for Investment

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` First of all, the applicant has to find a suitable business project to invest in. They are usually new commercial enterprises or regional center projects. The applicant can directly invest in a new commercial enterprise. It should be an amount of either $1,000,000 or $800,000 if it is located in a Targeted Employment Area (TEA).
- `<p>` The investor has complete control of the business in direct investment. When investing via a regional center that USCIS has officially appointed, the investors pool their funds to finance bigger projects, which are normally in TEAs and meet the job creation requirements.

## SECTION 7 — Two Year Conditional Permanent Residency

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` The third step of the EB-5 application is the applicant becoming a two-year conditional resident of the US, so they can implement the project was funded by their investment. Once USCIS approves the I-526 petition, the EB-5 investors are eligible to become US residents.
- `<p>` There are two ways to attain residency:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>The EB-5 investor, if he already possesses a lawful status in the United States, should file form I-485 to adjust their status to conditional permanent resident.</span>
- `<li>` <span>If the investor does not have lawful status in the US, they should file for an immigrant visa by submitting DS-260 to the National Visa Center. It should proceed through the US consulate or embassy in their native country.</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 8 — Unconditional Permanent Residency

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` The final step of the EB5 visa process involves the applicants becoming unconditional permanent residents by removing their two-year conditional status. The I-829 petition has to be submitted to the USCIS 90 days before the second anniversary of the date the applicant first obtained the conditional residency.
- `<p>` This application serves as proof that the investor has fulfilled all the requirements of the EB-5 visa. After submitting the I-829, the USCIS usually issues a permanent green card after 22 to 45 months.
