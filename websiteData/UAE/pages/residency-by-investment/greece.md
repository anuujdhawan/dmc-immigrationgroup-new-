## SECTION 2 — Changes in Greece Golden Visa Program

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The Greek Golden Visa is one of the most popular and preferred options to acquire European residency by investment program. Earlier this year, the Greek government revised the program. This represents some changes for possible investors. The minimum investment threshold of the Greek Golden Visa Program inceased from €250,000 to €800,000 in certain areas. The changes will become effective as of September 1st, 2024. This increased limit applies to real estate investments in region of Attica, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, and Thira and on islands having populations over 3,100 residents.
- `<p>` If you have already submitted an application for Greece Golden Visa, your case will be under review. So, the new changes shall not impact your application. The investments the investors made under the previous regulations will go as planned.
