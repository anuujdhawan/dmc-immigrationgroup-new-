## SECTION 5 — How Does the Canadian SUW Work?

> tag: `<h2>`

`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>For permanent residence, the applicant needs to get a letter of support from one of the designated investor organizations. The letter shall state they would fund the business. This letter is needed to file his/her PR.</span>
- `<li>` <span>Applicant should be able to communicate in English, French, or both. They need to provide proof of language proficiency. You need to score a minimum level of 5 in English or French.</span>
- `<li>` <span>A single business may be owned by a maximum of five owners when applying for the SUV program. Each applicant must possess at least 10% of voting rights. </span>
- `<li>` <span>Applicant needs to prove proof to the Canadian government that they have sufficient funds to support themselves and their family in Canada. There is no government financial support for entrepreneurial immigrants. </span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 7 — Eligibility Criteria for Investing in Canada for Citizenship – Start-Up Visa Program

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` If you are an entrepreneur with a unique idea, then your out-of-the-box thinking can make you eligible for Canada residency by investment via Start-Up visa program.
- `<p>` Here are the eligibility requirements an applicant is required to fulfill:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>The applicant should be in good health</span>
- `<li>` <span>No criminal record</span>
- `<li>` <span>Proficiency in English or French language at IELTS/CLB Level 5</span>
- `<li>` <span>Foreign nationals need to get a Letter of Support or investment commitment from one of the Designated Organizations in Canada</span>
- `<li>` <span>Each of the foreign national should own at least 10% of the shares in the startup</span>

`</ul>`
