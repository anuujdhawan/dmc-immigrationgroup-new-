## SECTION 2 — Who Needs A Czech Schengen Visa?

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` Czech is a part of the European Union and follows the same visa policies as the other Schengen countries. So, all the UAE residents or expats who are from a third country, like Pakistan, Russia, or India, need to obtain a Czech visa to enter the country. It is a short-stay Schengen tourist visa that is issued to foreign travellers for business purposes, to explore tourist areas, get medical treatments, religious reasons, and to participate in cultural or sports events.

## SECTION 6 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">Do I need a visa to visit the Czech Republic from the UAE?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` If you are a UAE citizen, you don’t need a visa for short visits, up to 90 days, to the Czech Republic or other Schengen countries. However, if you are a UAE resident but not a citizen, you’ll need to apply for a Schengen short-stay visa before travelling.

## SECTION 7 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">What type of visa should I apply for?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` For tourism, family visits, business trips, or religious reasons, you’ll need a Schengen Type C visit visa. It allows you to stay in the Czech Republic and other Schengen countries for up to 90 days within a 180-day period.

## SECTION 8 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">How long does it take to process the visa?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` The processing time usually takes about 15 calendar days from the date of your appointment. However, during busy travel seasons, it might take a little longer, so it’s best to apply at least 3 to 4 weeks in advance.

## SECTION 9 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">What documents do I need for the Czech Republic visit visa?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` You’ll need a valid passport, a completed application form, passport photos, flight and hotel bookings, travel insurance, bank statements, and a UAE residence visa copy. You may also need an invitation letter if you’re visiting family or attending a special event.

## SECTION 10 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">How much does the visa cost?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` The Czech visit visa fee is usually around EUR 80, which you can pay in AED at the visa application center. There might also be a small service charge. Children under 6 years old are exempt from paying the visa fee.

## SECTION 11 — <span> </span> <a href="" class="elementor-accordion-title">Can I visit other countries in Europe with this visa?</a>

> tag: `<h4>`

- `<p>` Yes, you can. The Czech visit visa is a Schengen visa, which means you can travel freely to other Schengen countries during your stay, such as Germany, Austria, France, Italy, and more.
