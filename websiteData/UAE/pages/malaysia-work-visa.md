## SECTION 5 — Employment Pass

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` An employment pass or EP is the most commonly issued work visa to foreign professionals hired by Malaysian companies. It allows you to work for a specific employer for a specific duration. In case of changing employer your current EP will expire and your new employer will have to apply for a new pass.
- `<p>` Employment pass usually has a 1-5 years validity, extendable, and can be classified into three categories.
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>Category 1: it has a 5 years validity and is issued to employees receiving a minimum monthly salary of RM 10,000. You can easily renew it. </span>
- `<li>` <span>Category 2: employees with a minimum salary of RM 5000-9000 can apply for a type 2 EP. It's renewable and lasts for 23 months only. This type of visa holder can bring their dependents and helpers with them, </span>
- `<li>` <span>Category 3: mid-level skilled workers who are earning RM 3000-5000. It is valid only for 1 year or 12 months only.</span>
`</ul>`

## SECTION 6 — Digital Nomad Visa

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` It is a part of Malaysia’s government “De Return Program” and issued only to international freelancers and remote workers who are offering their services to Malaysian companies. It allows you to stay in the country for 3-12 months and can be easily renewed.
- `<p>` Digital Nomad visa allows you to bring immediate family members to Malaysia. However, you need to clearly mention the names of dependents in the visa application and pay an additional RM 500 for each member.

## SECTION 7 — Temporary Employment Pass

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` It is a temporary visa that has a 12 month validity and can be extended every year for10 years. It is specifically designed for semi-skilled workers living in Pakistan, India, or any other country. TEP visa holders are not allowed to change the employer and the sector.

## SECTION 8 — Professional Visit Pass

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` Professional visit pass is another type of <b>Malaysia work visa for Pakistani</b>, Indian, and other country professionals who want to work in a Malaysian company. It is valid for 12 months only and does not allow you to bring any dependent or helper with you!

## SECTION 9 — Pls@Xpats or Social Visit Pass

> tag: `<h3>`

- `<p>` It is a short term visa issued to foreign nationals, allowing them to work in Malaysia for 30 days. This visa is only for government and some specific private sector employees such as healthcare, aviation, and business services.
