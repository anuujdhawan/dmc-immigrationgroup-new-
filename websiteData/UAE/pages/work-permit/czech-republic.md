## SECTION 5 — Eligibility Criteria to Apply for a Czech Republic Work Permit Visa

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The Czech Republic has an open-door policy for skilled immigrants from non-EU countries, with only a few restrictions. A work permit is not required for citizens of EU countries or those from certain non-EU countries who are on an intra-company transfer or are in possession of a valid long-term residence permit. Citizens from other non-EU countries must apply for a work permit before taking up employment in the Czech Republic. Listed are some of the eligibility criteria that applicants will need to fulfill:
`<ul>`
- `<li>` <span>A completed application form</span>
- `<li>` <span>A document that confirms your education level or qualifications (if applicable)</span>
- `<li>` <span>Your passport and ID card copy</span>
- `<li>` <span>Should prove the case officer that they will leave the country once their work permit expires</span>
- `<li>` <span>An employment contract with your name and address on it</span>
- `<li>` <span>A document that confirms your health insurance coverage (if applicable)</span>
- `<li>` <span>Relevant work experience and training sessions to perform the job</span>
- `<li>` <span>Should have a clean police criminal record</span>
`</ul>`
