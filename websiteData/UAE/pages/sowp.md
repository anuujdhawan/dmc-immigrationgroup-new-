## SECTION 1 — What is SOWP in Canada?

> tag: `<h2>`

- `<p>` The SOWP full form is Spousal Open Work Permit. It is a special program that allows spouses or partners of foreign nationals working or studying in Canada to join them and work legally. Through SOWP, open work permit holders can work for any employer across Canada without restrictions.
- `<p>` It’s a great option for family members of a principal applicant who already holds a valid study or work permit. This program is especially beneficial for most foreign workers from Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and other emirates across the UAE who wish to reunite with their loved ones. It also supports Canada’s effort to fill labour shortages across various industries.
- `<p>` Whether you’re married or in a common-law union, you may qualify for the SOWP as long as your partner in Canada maintains a valid status. This program makes it easier for couples to live, work, and grow together in Canada.

**Q** `<h4>` — Can I apply for SOWP inside Canada?

**A** `<p>` — Yes, you can. It allows you to maintain your legal stay while your application is being processed.

**Q** `<h4>` — Is SOWP the same as a bridging open work permit?

**A** `<p>` — No, they are different. The SOWP is specifically designed for spouses or common-law partners of foreign workers or students. Meanwhile, a Bridging Open Work Permit (BOWP) is for individuals who have already applied for permanent residence and are waiting for a decision.

**Q** `<h4>` — How long can I stay in Canada on a SOWP?

**A** `<p>` — Your stay usually matches the validity of your spouse’s study or work permit. For example, if your partner’s permit is valid for two years, your SOWP will typically have the same duration. You must renew your permit before it expires if you wish to stay longer.

**Q** `<h4>` — Can I extend my spousal open work permit?

**A** `<p>` — Yes, you can, but only when your spouse or partner continues to hold a valid permit. It’s important to apply for an extension before your current permit expires to maintain your legal status. You can contact us to get the complete spouse open work permit extension document checklist and assistance for the application process.

**Q** `<h4>` — Can I include my dependent children in the SOWP application?

**A** `<p>` — According to the new rules, you cannot add them to your application. If they want to move with you, they can apply separately under a relevant category.

**Q** `<h4>` — What's the average SOWP Canada processing time?

**A** `<p>` — It can vary depending on where you apply from and the volume of applications received by IRCC. On average, it takes about 3 to 6 months for most cases to be processed. However, applications submitted with complete documentation and biometrics are often approved faster.
